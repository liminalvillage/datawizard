*Annex 3 to the [Asset Use Agreement (Comodato d'Uso)](../Agreement%201%20%E2%80%94%20Asset%20Use.md). Sets out the division of maintenance responsibilities between the Association and the Owner. Forms an integral part of that Agreement. Also referenced in the [Stewardship Agreement](../Agreement%202%20%E2%80%94%20Stewardship.md) (Clause 10).*

---

## Section A — Definitions

**Ordinary maintenance:** Routine upkeep needed to preserve the Asset in good working condition through normal use. Responsibility of the **Association** during periods of use (pro rata where relevant).

**Extraordinary maintenance:** Substantial repairs or replacements due to structural issues, wear beyond ordinary use, ageing infrastructure, or unforeseen damage. Responsibility of the **Owner**, to be carried out within a reasonable time after notification by the Association.

---

## Section B — Ordinary Maintenance (Association's Responsibility)

The following tasks are the responsibility of the Association during its periods of use:

**Cleaning and tidying (see also [A1-4 — Cleaning and Restoration Protocol](A1-4%20%E2%80%94%20Cleaning%20and%20Restoration%20Protocol.md)):**
- Daily common area tidying during stays and events
- Kitchen cleaning after each use (surfaces, appliances, floor)
- Bathroom cleaning after each use (toilets, showers, sinks, floors)
- End-of-stay deep clean of all areas used

**Grounds and outdoor spaces:**
- Grass cutting and pathway maintenance: [frequency — e.g. weekly during growing season]
- Weeding of kitchen garden and paths: [e.g. as needed]
- Removal of waste and compost management
- Pruning of hedges and small trees (not structural pruning): [e.g. once annually]

**Minor indoor repairs:**
- Replacing lightbulbs and fuses
- Minor plumbing (unblocking sinks, replacing tap washers)
- Touching up paintwork after minor scuffs or marks
- Re-hanging doors or cupboard fronts that have come loose
- Sealing minor tile cracks (cosmetic only — not structural)
- Replacing broken window catches (not frames)

**Equipment and tools:**
- Routine cleaning of shared tools after use
- Sharpening of cutting tools (scythes, pruning shears)
- Basic lubrication and maintenance of mechanical equipment

**Utilities management:**
- Monitoring energy and water consumption
- Reporting unusual consumption patterns to the Association coordinator
- Ensuring all utilities are switched off / secured at end of each use period

**Reporting obligation:** The Association shall notify the Owner promptly (and in any event within [e.g. 5 business days]) of any defect, damage, or maintenance need that goes beyond ordinary maintenance.

---

## Section C — Extraordinary Maintenance (Owner's Responsibility)

The following are examples of extraordinary maintenance that remain the Owner's responsibility and cost:

- Roof repair or replacement
- Structural wall repair or consolidation
- Foundation work
- Full replacement of boiler, heating system, or water heater
- Full replacement of electrical wiring or distribution board
- Full window or door frame replacement
- Septic tank or drainage system overhaul
- Major tree surgery (felling, heavy pruning of large trees)
- Replacement of flooring (not cosmetic repair)

**Response time after notification:** The Owner commits to commencing extraordinary maintenance within [e.g. 30] days of receiving written notification from the Association, except in cases of genuine unavailability (in which case an agreed timeline shall be communicated promptly).

---

## Section D — Shared and Ambiguous Cases

Where a maintenance task falls on the boundary between ordinary and extraordinary, the Parties shall determine responsibility in good faith based on: the cause of the need (normal use vs. structural failure), the cost involved, and the urgency. If no agreement is reached within [e.g. 15] days, the dispute resolution procedure under GT-7 of the General Terms applies.

---

## Section E — Maintenance Log

The Association (or its steward) shall maintain a simple maintenance log for the Asset, recording:

| Date | Task | Carried out by | Cost (if any) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [DD/MM/YYYY] | [description] | [name / role] | [€ amount or "nil"] | |

This log shall be shared with the Owner upon request and reviewed at each periodic review meeting (Stewardship Agreement, Clause 14.2).
