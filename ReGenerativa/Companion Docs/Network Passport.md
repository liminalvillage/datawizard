## What the Network Passport Is

Every person who contributes to this network — as a member, steward, mentor, supporter, partner, or intrapreneur — accumulates a single, portable record of their contributions. That record travels with them across roles, across hubs, across time. It does not reset. It does not expire. It grows.

The Network Passport is that record. It lives in the Contribution Ledger (GT-3) and belongs entirely to the person who holds it. It is theirs to share, to carry into new roles, to present as evidence of who they are in this network — not who they claim to be, but who they have shown themselves to be, validated by others, over time.

The Passport is the network's answer to a question that relational economies have always struggled with at scale: *how do you carry trust with you when you move?* In a village, reputation travels by word of mouth. In a network of villages, it needs a container. The Passport is that container.

## The Four Tiers

Tiers follow the logic of ecological succession: continuous, cumulative growth through recognisable stages. There are no hard gates — no moment at which a person is suddenly a different thing. There is only the gradual deepening of presence, validated by the community that has witnessed it.

### 🌱 Seed
*Arriving. First contributions germinating.*

The Seed has crossed a threshold — signed an agreement, entered a hub, begun to contribute. Their presence is new and their record is forming. The network receives them with welcome and curiosity. What they bring is potential, not yet proven.

### 🌿 Sprout
*Establishing. First roots taking hold.*

The Sprout has built a validated contribution record. They have shown up, followed through, and been witnessed doing so by others. The network begins to know them — not just their name, but their way of working, their reliability, their particular gifts. Trust is forming.

### 🌳 Branch
*Extending. Shaping the ecosystem around them.*

The Branch is a known presence in the network. They have contributed across contexts — different hubs, different roles, different seasons. They have navigated difficulty and stayed. Their contributions have shaped things: programmes have happened, land has been tended, ventures have formed. The network is different because they are in it.

### 🌲 Canopy
*Full presence. The network grows in their shade.*

The Canopy is a long-term sustainer of the network's life. Their contributions span years, not seasons. They carry institutional memory. Younger participants grow in the conditions the Canopy has created. Their Passport is the network's story told through one person's arc.

## Tier Thresholds

Specific thresholds — the contribution volumes at which a participant moves from Seed to Sprout, Sprout to Branch, and Branch to Canopy — are defined by the Association's governance body and published as a living governance resolution.

Thresholds are not fixed in these agreements deliberately. The Contribution Ledger is under active development, and the right thresholds will only become clear once the network has enough data to see what meaningful contribution actually looks like across roles, seasons, and contexts. Premature thresholds create the wrong incentives.

**Principles the governance body shall apply when setting thresholds:**
- Thresholds should be achievable by a genuinely active participant within a reasonable period — not so demanding that only extraordinary contributors advance
- Thresholds should be multi-dimensional — hours alone are insufficient; quality, diversity of contribution, and peer validation should all count
- Thresholds should be reviewed seasonally and adjusted as the network learns
- A participant who disagrees with their tier assessment may request a review through the Collegio dei Probiviri or equivalent governance body

## Role-Scoped Perks

The same tier unlocks different things depending on which agreement(s) a participant holds. Perks are cumulative — a Canopy holds all Sprout and Branch perks within each role.

### As a Member (Agreement 3)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| Seed | Standard membership rights and access |
| Sprout | Discounted access to Mentorship Programmes; priority booking at hubs |
| Branch | Free access to Mentorship Programmes; nomination rights for governance positions |
| Canopy | Lifetime membership option (fee waived by Board resolution); named in the network's founding story and living history |

### As a Steward (Agreement 2)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| Seed | Standard stewardship terms |
| Sprout | Preferred placement when multiple candidates apply for stewardship of a hub; network introduction when seeking new stewardship roles |
| Branch | 90-day termination notice protection regardless of tenure duration; right to be consulted when incoming owner negotiations affect the hub; named in network hub records for the duration of stewardship |
| Canopy | Named stewardship legacy permanently recorded on the hub's history; formal consultation right on revisions to the Hub DNA of any hub they have stewarded |

### As a Mentor (Agreement 4)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| Seed | Standard mentorship terms |
| Sprout | Increased free-place allocation per programme; featured listing in network mentor directory |
| Branch | Co-design input rights on the network's programme calendar; preferred hub access for programme delivery |
| Canopy | Named knowledge custodian status; legacy programme designation (a programme permanently associated with the mentor's name in network records) |

### As a Supporter (Agreement 5)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| Seed | Standard supporter recognition |
| Sprout | Named in Association annual report; invited to seasonal network gatherings |
| Branch | Named infrastructure element at a supported hub (a garden, tool library, or shared space named in their honour) |
| Canopy | Network patron recognition; named in the founding story and living history of the network |

### As a Partner (Agreement 6)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| Seed | Standard partnership terms |
| Sprout | Expedited contribution validation process; priority consideration in project lead selection |
| Branch | Trusted Partner status — simplified onboarding for new projects; reduced administrative overhead |
| Canopy | Elder Partner status — formally consulted on significant network commercial decisions; advisory input into partnership governance evolution |

### As an Intrapreneur (Agreement 7)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| Seed | Standard intrapreneurship terms; Phase 2 base NRC rate of 7% |
| Sprout | Phase 2 NRC rate reduced to 5% (from base 7%) — matching the standard network rate |
| Branch | Expedited Phase Transition Review process; preferred fiscal hosting arrangements |
| Canopy | Reduced post-graduation NRC obligation period (as defined in the [[A7-3 — Graduation Agreement]]) |

*Note on NRC rates: The Phase 2 base rate of 7% reflects the Association's continued ecosystem support during early external trading. Reaching Sprout tier — demonstrating established and validated contribution — brings the rate to the standard 5%. Phase 3 is 5% for all. This structure rewards demonstrated commitment rather than penalising early external activity.*

## The Passport at Transitions

When a participant exits a role — for any reason — the Association provides a written Passport summary for that role within 30 days (GT-3A.6). This summary includes:

- The role held and the period of service
- Key contributions validated during the period
- Tier reached at exit
- Any named legacies, recognition, or infrastructure associated with the participant

The summary is the participant's to share as they choose. It is not a reference letter — it is a contribution record. It speaks for itself.

When a participant enters a new role or arrives at a new hub, they may share their Passport (or relevant portions of it) with the receiving community. The receiving community is not obligated to grant perks from a previous tier until they have validated new contributions — but they are strongly encouraged to extend welcome proportionate to the Passport's depth.

## Passport and GDPR

The Network Passport is subject to all data protection provisions in GT-5 and GT-3.4. Participants may:
- Request a full export of their Passport data at any time
- Request correction of any validated contribution record through the governance body
- Request cryptographic unlinking of their on-chain identifier (right to erasure) — noting that this will render historical on-chain records unattributable but does not delete the underlying network activity

The Association maintains the mapping between pseudonymised on-chain identifiers and real identities off-chain, as data controller, under appropriate security measures.
