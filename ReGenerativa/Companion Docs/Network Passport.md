## What the Network Passport Is

Every person who contributes to this network — as a member, owner, steward, mentor, supporter, partner, intrapreneur, or elder — accumulates a single, portable record of their contributions. That record travels with them across roles, across hubs, across time. It does not reset. It does not expire. It grows.

The Network Passport is that record. It lives in the Contribution Ledger (GT-3) and belongs entirely to the person who holds it. It is theirs to share, to carry into new roles, to present as evidence of who they are in this network — not who they claim to be, but who they have shown themselves to be, validated by others, over time.

The Passport is the network's answer to a question that relational economies have always struggled with at scale: *how do you carry trust with you when you move?* In a village, reputation travels by word of mouth. In a network of villages, it needs a container. The Passport is that container.

## The Four Tiers

Tiers follow a developmental arc from individual to ecology. The first three are continuous: each grows from the one before through accumulated, validated contribution. The fourth — Forest — is qualitatively different: it is conferred, not accumulated, and marks the passage from individual contribution to ecological role.

### 🌱 Seed
*Arriving. Germination. First contributions taking root.*

The Seed has crossed a threshold — signed an agreement, entered a hub, begun to contribute. Their presence is new and their record is forming. The network receives them with welcome and curiosity. What they bring is potential, not yet proven.

### 🌿 Sprout
*Establishing. Growing into the role. First canopy forming.*

The Sprout has built a validated contribution record. They have shown up, followed through, and been witnessed doing so by others. The network begins to know them — not just their name, but their way of working, their reliability, their particular gifts. Trust is forming.

### 🌳 Tree
*Mature presence. Full vigour. The network gathers in their shade and grows from their seeds.*

The Tree is a long-term sustainer of the network's life. They have contributed across contexts — different hubs, different roles, different seasons — and stayed. Their contributions have shaped things: programmes have happened, land has been tended, ventures have formed. Younger participants grow in the conditions they have created. The network is different because they are in it.

### 🌲 Forest
*The Elder. The ecology in which new Seeds germinate. The network's deep holding.*

The Forest is no longer an individual contributor. They have become the *conditions* for others — shade, soil, mycelium, the relational substrate from which the next generation can root. Forest is conferred through the Transition Rite under [[Agreement 8 — Eldership]] and brings formal governance standing in the **Elder Council**, unconditional hospitality at all hubs, and permanent inscription in the network's living history. The Elder *is* the forest.

## Tier Thresholds

Specific thresholds — the contribution volumes at which a participant moves from Seed to Sprout, and Sprout to Tree — are defined by the Association's governance body and published as a living governance resolution. The transition from Tree to Forest is not threshold-based; it is governed by Agreement 8.

Thresholds are not fixed in these agreements deliberately. The Contribution Ledger is under active development, and the right thresholds will only become clear once the network has enough data to see what meaningful contribution actually looks like across roles, seasons, and contexts. Premature thresholds create the wrong incentives.

**Principles the governance body shall apply when setting thresholds:**
- Thresholds should be achievable by a genuinely active participant within a reasonable period — not so demanding that only extraordinary contributors advance
- Thresholds should be multi-dimensional — hours alone are insufficient; quality, diversity of contribution, and peer validation should all count
- Thresholds should be reviewed seasonally and adjusted as the network learns
- A participant who disagrees with their tier assessment may request a review through the **Elder Council** (or, until it is constituted, the Collegio dei Probiviri) per GT-7.2

## Role-Scoped Perks

The same tier unlocks different things depending on which agreement(s) a participant holds. Perks are cumulative — a Tree holds all Sprout perks within each role.

### As a Member (Agreement 2)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard membership rights and access |
| 🌿 Sprout | Discounted access to Mentorship Programmes; priority booking at hubs |
| 🌳 Tree | Free access to Mentorship Programmes; nomination rights for governance positions; lifetime membership option (fee waived by Board resolution); named in the network's living history |
| 🌲 Forest | Annual membership fee permanently waived (Agreement 8 §4.3); formal seat on the **Elder Council** with the authorities defined in Agreement 8 §5 |

### As an Owner (Agreement 1)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard ownership terms; recognition in hub records |
| 🌿 Sprout | Named in network communications; invited to seasonal owner gatherings; cross-hub introductions |
| 🌳 Tree | Voice in cross-hub network coordination; named permanently in hub legacy; consultative role in network strategic decisions affecting Owner concerns |
| 🌲 Forest | Recognition as patron of the network commons; Elder Council seat per Agreement 8 |

### As a Steward (Agreement 3)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard stewardship terms |
| 🌿 Sprout | Preferred placement when multiple candidates apply for stewardship of a hub; network introduction when seeking new stewardship roles |
| 🌳 Tree | 90-day termination notice protection (per A3 §16.1); right to be consulted when incoming owner negotiations affect the hub; named stewardship legacy permanently recorded on the hub's history; formal consultation right on revisions to the Hub DNA of any hub they have stewarded |
| 🌲 Forest | Unconditional hospitality at all hubs (Agreement 8 §4.1); active stewardship obligations released; informal mentorship to active stewards by choice |

### As a Mentor (Agreement 4)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard mentorship terms |
| 🌿 Sprout | Increased free-place allocation per programme; featured listing in network mentor directory |
| 🌳 Tree | Co-design input rights on the network's programme calendar; preferred hub access for programme delivery; named knowledge custodian status; legacy programme designation (a programme permanently associated with the mentor's name) |
| 🌲 Forest | Honorary teacher status — no programme delivery obligation; informal transmission only; named in the network's wisdom lineage |

### As a Supporter (Agreement 5)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard supporter recognition |
| 🌿 Sprout | Named in Association annual report; invited to seasonal network gatherings |
| 🌳 Tree | Named infrastructure element at a supported hub (a garden, tool library, or shared space named in their honour); network patron recognition; named in the founding story and living history |
| 🌲 Forest | Permanent named legacy across the network; Elder Council consultation rights on significant resource flow decisions |

### As a Partner (Agreement 6)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard partnership terms |
| 🌿 Sprout | Expedited contribution validation process; priority consideration in project lead selection |
| 🌳 Tree | Trusted Partner status — simplified onboarding for new projects; reduced administrative overhead; advisory input into partnership governance evolution |
| 🌲 Forest | Full Elder Partner standing — formal Elder Council consultation rights when commercial decisions intersect with the commons |

### As an Intrapreneur (Agreement 7)

| Tier | Perks |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Standard intrapreneurship terms; Phase 2 base NRC rate of 7% |
| 🌿 Sprout | Phase 2 NRC rate reduced to 5% (from base 7%) — matching the standard network rate |
| 🌳 Tree | Expedited Phase Transition Review process; preferred fiscal hosting arrangements; reduced post-graduation NRC obligation period (as defined in the [[Agreement 7 Annex 3 — Graduation|Graduation Agreement]]) |
| 🌲 Forest | Forest Intrapreneur status — presumed to serve as venture mentor and Elder witness, not active lead; honorary recognition in graduated entities they helped seed |

*Note on NRC rates: The Phase 2 base rate of 7% reflects the Association's continued ecosystem support during early external trading. Reaching Sprout tier — demonstrating established and validated contribution — brings the rate to the standard 5%. Phase 3 is 5% for all. This structure rewards demonstrated commitment rather than penalising early external activity.*

## The Passport at Transitions

When a participant exits a role — for any reason — the Association provides a written Passport summary for that role within 30 days (GT-3A.6). This summary includes:

- The role held and the period of service
- Key contributions validated during the period
- Tier reached at exit
- Any named legacies, recognition, or infrastructure associated with the participant

The summary is the participant's to share as they choose. It is not a reference letter — it is a contribution record. It speaks for itself.

When a participant enters a new role or arrives at a new hub, they may share their Passport (or relevant portions of it) with the receiving community. The receiving community is not obligated to grant perks from a previous tier until they have validated new contributions — but they are strongly encouraged to extend welcome proportionate to the Passport's depth.

## Passport and GDPR

The Network Passport is subject to all data protection provisions in GT-5 and GT-3.4. Participants may:
- Request a full export of their Passport data at any time
- Request correction of any validated contribution record through the governance body
- Request cryptographic unlinking of their on-chain identifier (right to erasure) — noting that this will render historical on-chain records unattributable but does not delete the underlying network activity

The Association maintains the mapping between pseudonymised on-chain identifiers and real identities off-chain, as data controller, under appropriate security measures.
