## 2026-05-11 — Reversal: A1 and A6 annex numbering normalised (gaps closed)

**Decision:** Renumber the surviving annexes in Agreement 1 and Agreement 6 to close the gaps left by the 2026-04-14 deletion of superseded annexes. Final state is contiguous numbering across all 18 annexes in the constellation: A1-1 through A1-5; A2-1; A3-1; A4-1; A5-1 to A5-2; A6-1 through A6-4; A7-1 through A7-3; A8-1.

**Decided by:** Holons (vault owner) in session 2026-05-11. Executed by Claude under DataWizard protocol.

**Renames:** A1-5 Shared Infrastructure → A1-4; A1-6 Hub DNA → A1-5; A6-3 Economics Schedule → A6-2; A6-4 Project Sheet → A6-3; A6-6 Validation Platform → A6-4.

**Reversal of:** the 2026-04-14 decision (logged in Session Log of that date) to preserve the gaps left by superseded-annex deletion. That decision was made on cost-vs-benefit grounds: closing the gaps would have required touching many wikilinks, and the benefit was deemed cosmetic.

**Why reversed.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **Cosmetic stops being cosmetic at signing.** The constellation is approaching a state where it will be signed and filed by counterparties — APS-ETS registration, hub owners, partners, sponsors. Non-contiguous numbering ("A1-3, A1-5, A1-6") creates the impression of missing pages, especially in a legal document. A counterparty who has not lived the history of the consolidation will simply see a gap and ask. The cost of explaining the gap repeatedly, in every counterparty interaction, exceeds the one-time cost of closing it now.

2. **The cost was overestimated in April.** The actual reference graph — once mapped — was manageable: 20 cross-references across 13 files, executed in two chunks under one hour, with all changes surgical (`patch_note`, not full rewrites). The work that looked like "many wikilinks" was bounded.

3. **Stability is now the right time.** No further annex consolidations are anticipated. Closing the gaps now establishes contiguous numbering as the reference state going forward; closing them later (after signing begins) becomes increasingly expensive in proportion to the number of executed copies in circulation.

**Alternatives considered and rejected:**

- **Continuing to preserve gaps with a footnote explaining the history.** Rejected. Footnotes do not survive the act of reading a 30-page legal document in one sitting. The numbering must read clearly on first encounter.

- **Renumbering only A6 (where the gaps are more visible) and leaving A1's single gap.** Rejected. Partial normalisation creates a worse problem than no normalisation: a reader sees A6 is contiguous but A1 is not, and concludes that the A1 gap is meaningful when it is simply unaddressed.

**Consequences executed in the same session:** Detailed in Session Log entry 2026-05-11. Twenty cross-references swept across thirteen files, plus five file renames and frontmatter updates.

**Reversibility.** Reversible if the network later judges this decision wrong — the operation is symmetric. As of this entry no signed copies exist.

**Note on the 2026-04-14 decision.** The April decision was not wrong at the time; it was made on the information then available and with no near-term signing pressure. This reversal does not invalidate the prior reasoning — it updates the cost-benefit calculation in light of the constellation now approaching operational readiness.

## 2026-05-10 — Membership becomes A2; Stewardship becomes A3

**Decision:** Renumber the constellation so that Membership is Agreement 2 and Stewardship is Agreement 3. New order: A1 Ownership → A2 Membership → A3 Stewardship → A4 Mentorship → A5 Sponsorship → A6 Partnership → A7 Intrapreneurship → A8 Eldership.

**Decided by:** Holons (vault owner) in session 2026-05-10. Executed by Claude under DataWizard protocol.

**Context.** Until this decision, Membership was Agreement 3 and Stewardship was Agreement 2. The Stewardship-before-Membership ordering had reflected an older architectural lens in which the operational layer (the steward who holds a hub) was foregrounded over the gateway layer (the member who joins). In practice — confirmed by Agreement 3's own Clause 1.4 ("Membership is a prerequisite for natural persons holding any active role in the ReGenerativa constellation") and by GT-11.4 — Membership is the gateway. No natural person can hold any other active agreement without first being a Member. The constellation's own logic placed Membership before Stewardship; the numbering did not.

**Rationale.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **Numbering should reflect the prerequisite chain.** Membership is the threshold every natural person crosses first. Stewardship is one of several roles that becomes available once that threshold has been crossed. A reader encountering the agreements in order should encounter the gateway before encountering the role.

2. **The Architecture of Belonging cascade reads more truly with the new order.** The Introduction's "From X comes Y" narrative arc — place → belonging → presence → expertise → support → collaboration → creation — is the deepening sequence the network actually follows. A person finds the place, chooses to belong, may then deepen into stewardship, etc. The previous numbering forced the cascade to invert at the second step (place → presence → belonging) which read as a logical jolt rather than a deepening.

3. **The numbering creates legibility for new readers.** A first-time reader of the constellation should be able to learn the architecture from the order itself. Membership (A2) before Stewardship (A3) makes the gateway prerequisite immediately visible. Stewardship (A2) before Membership (A3) required a separate explanation.

**Alternatives considered and rejected:**

- **Leaving the numbering as-is and adding more cross-references to clarify the prerequisite.** Rejected. The constellation already had several places (GT-11.4, A3 Clause 1.4, Cheatsheet "Prerequisite chain" line) explaining that Membership came before Stewardship logically. Adding more such notes would treat the symptom, not the cause. Numbering carries semantic weight; if the numbering misleads, the cure is to fix the numbering.

- **Inserting Membership as A0 or A1.5 to preserve all other numbers.** Rejected. Non-standard numbering creates more friction than it saves. A clean integer sequence from 1 to 8 is the most legible option for legal documents that will eventually be signed and filed by counterparties unfamiliar with the constellation's internal history.

- **Performing the swap in a future cleanup pass rather than now.** Rejected. The longer the inverted numbering persists, the more downstream artefacts (signed agreements, decision references, external citations) will calcify around it. None of those exist yet at scale; the cost of swapping is finite and bounded today.

**Consequences executed in the same session:**

- Two file renames (with intermediate temporary names to avoid filename collisions on Obsidian's filesystem)
- Two annex renames following the same pattern (A2-1 Steward Onboarding becomes A3-1 Steward Onboarding; A3-1 Member Onboarding becomes A2-1 Member Onboarding)
- Frontmatter `parent_agreement` and `title` updates on both renamed annexes
- Body cross-reference sweep across:
  - All 8 agreements (A2-A3 wikilinks, parenthetical "(Agreement N)" references in A8)
  - 4 affected annexes (the two renamed onboarding annexes, plus A1-3 Hub Care Protocol and A1-6 Hub DNA which reference Stewardship)
  - General Terms GT-11.3 constellation list (reordered)
  - Constellation Cheatsheet (Eight Agreements table, Prerequisite chain note, Key Numbers table, Annexes-at-a-Glance table)
  - Introduction narrative (Architecture of Belonging cascade rewritten in new order; "Each part sustains the whole" sentences rewritten to match)
  - Network Passport companion (role-perk table headers; A2/A3 § references in Steward perks)
  - Operational Procedures companion (Section A and B headers + all internal § references; Migration Roadmap)
  - Social DNA Framework companion (Stewardship reference)
  - Project Guidelines (Six Agreements → Eight Agreements table in new order; folder structure listing; Constellation definition; Social DNA domain names corrected to canonical GT-1.6 list)
  - MOC (Agreements table rows swapped; Annexes section subsections swapped)

**Consequences pending:**

- Wikilinks in MOC use a shorthand convention (e.g. `[[A2-1 — Member Onboarding Record]]` rather than full filename `[[Agreement 2 Annex 1 — Member Onboarding]]`). The convention was preserved in this session but not normalised; some links may not resolve cleanly in Obsidian. A future session should decide whether to standardise on full filenames or maintain the shorthand convention with explicit aliases.
- The Operational Procedures Migration Roadmap continues to list Membership as A2 (correctly) but the migration sequence ordering itself was not reconsidered in light of the renumbering. A future session may decide whether the sequence (Mentorship → Intrapreneurship → Membership → Validation Platform) still makes sense or should be re-sorted.

**Reversibility.** Reversible if the network later judges this decision wrong — the operation is symmetric. The cost of reversal grows as signed copies of the agreements proliferate. As of this entry no signed copies exist.
