## 2026-06-02 — Owner fair-use access to activities at their Asset (pending F1)

**Decision.** Added **A1 §2.5**: an Owner holds fair-use, free-at-point-of-use access to the network and community activities hosted at their own Asset, on the footing of a contributing participant — subject to capacity, safety, and Asset DNA, and not displacing a booked participant or a Steward's operational needs. **Exception:** a Mentor's resource-cost Programme slots still recover their direct delivery cost (Agreement 4; GT-4) — the Mentor's labour is the gift, but their out-of-pocket cost is not waived. The access is personal and non-commercial (the Owner's commercial use stays under §2.1/§2.4).

**Why.** A1 §2 already secured the Owner's access to the *space* (reasonable access, personal use, emergency entry). It said nothing about access to what *happens* in the space — that was left to emerge indirectly from contribution standing (GT-3A) and Member status. Naming it makes the bargain explicit and fair: the one who unlocks the place can share in its commons life without a turnstile, while a Mentor who would otherwise be left out of pocket is protected.

**Where.** New A1 §2.5; A1 3.4 → 3.5; MOC synced.

**Status.** Drafted 2026-06-02 — **Pending F1**. On branch `holon-homeostat-strategy`.

## 2026-06-02 — Allocation value systems: each holon declares how its Ledger becomes shares (pending F1)

**Decision.** Specified the missing function in contribution-weighted allocation: a holon's discretionary-envelope shares are computed by applying the holon's **declared value system** to its Contribution Ledger entries (GT-1.7, GT-1.8). The Ledger records *what* was contributed; the value system sets *how* it is weighted. Each holon declares its own system **independently** as a **weighted combination of indicators** — anything it can measure or signal: validated hours, euros, declared roles, mutual-credit units of valued activity (e.g. trees planted, meals cooked), or qualitative collaboration signals, in whatever mix and weighting it chooses. Common configurations are egalitarian, hour-based, or role-based (which avoids continuous accounting). Network default absent a declaration: **egalitarian** among active co-Stewards.

**The gap.** GT-4.3, A2 §2.7, A3 §3.3, and LGR-5 §D all said shares were "in proportion to recorded active contribution" without ever defining how contribution becomes a number — leaving the most consequential step implicit. Naming it as a per-holon, declared parameter keeps subsidiarity (a tree-planting hub and a software venture can weigh differently) while the role-based option exists precisely so a holon can opt out of continuous accounting.

**Scope guard.** The value system governs the **allocation** reading only (the local discretionary share). It does not touch the network-wide **access** rule (GT-3A, one cumulative sliding scale) or the one-member-one-vote governance right (GT-11; A2 §2.1).

**Where.** New **GT-4.3A** (the substantive clause); GT-4.6 lists it as a per-holon parameter; LGR-5 §D records the menu + egalitarian default; A2 §2.7 and A3 §3.3 cross-reference it. Versions: GT 3.14 → 3.15, A2 3.5 → 3.6, A3 3.7 → 3.8, LGR 1.6 → 1.7. Cheatsheet and MOC synced.

**Status.** Drafted 2026-06-02 — **Pending F1**. On branch `holon-homeostat-strategy`.

## 2026-06-01 — Resolve the homeostat's Elder Council seam: recognition only (pending F1)

**Decision.** Closed the one genuinely unresolved design point of the holon homeostat. The third "return on feeding the common reservoir" — proposed as **network-tier standing** (a voice in how the Elder Council directs across-hub infrastructure) — is **resolved as recognition only**: a voluntary feed-up is recorded in the contributing Stewards' Network Passports as network-level contribution on the **lifetime-cumulative / access** reading, raising access standing and the Eldership-eligibility clock (LGR-1), but conferring **no allocation voice, no Council seat, and no governance vote**.

**The issue.** Feeding up earned two coherent local returns (mutual insurance, earned autonomy). The proposed third — a *voice in Council allocation* — collided with the Elder Council's honorific, **elder-based** composition (qualify by ~5 years' contribution per LGR-1, nomination, and the Transition Rite; "from doing to witnessing"). Enfranchising reservoir-feeders would either make the Council partly contribution-weighted (destroying its elder character and the governance/administration firewall) or force a whole new governance organ into being. The third return had nowhere coherent to live.

**Why recognition.** It needs no new machinery — it plugs into the two-readings Passport split already built (lifetime-cumulative = access/standing; active-recent = local allocation). Generosity routes to the *standing* reading, never the *allocation* one. Anti-gaming is intrinsic: feeding up trades local discretionary money for recognition + access standing, never for power anywhere; and starving your own holon to farm standing drops your local contribution.

**Alternatives considered and rejected.** *Advisory voice / consultation* — still a new standing channel touching governance, heavier than warranted. *Separate organ (stewards' chamber)* — a whole new bicameral body for one return; disproportionate. *Drop the third return entirely* — viable, but loses the "generosity is seen" property recognition cheaply preserves.

**Also fixed (defect).** While implementing, found that **A8 §4.2A "Common reservoir" had never actually landed** — the 2026-05-30 commit `b9c8550` changed only A8's frontmatter (v1.5→1.6); the clause body silently failed, though that commit's message and the logs claimed it was added. A8 §4.2A is now genuinely written (with the recognition resolution baked in), A8 → v1.7.

**Affects.** GT-4.8(d) (recognition added as a third return, explicitly no voice), LGR-5 §E (v1.6 — resolution replaces the "left open" note), A8 §4.2A (v1.7 — clause now exists), Constellation Cheatsheet, and the Holon Homeostat strategy note (open seam → resolved). One member, one vote and the elder-based Council both untouched.

**Status.** Drafted 2026-06-01 — **Pending F1**. On branch `holon-homeostat-strategy`. With this, the homeostat design has no open seams.

## 2026-05-30 — Membership = stewardship gradient + the holon homeostat (drafted, pending F1)

**Decision.** Drafted (not ratified) a coherent extension of the constellation in which **membership carries a standing, contribution-weighted stewardship mandate** and each holon runs as a **self-regulating living balance** over time. Full design rationale: [[Membership as Stewardship and the Common Reservoir - 2026-05-30]].

**Decided by:** Holons (vault owner), 2026-05-30, by directing "activate the DataWizard to change the agreements" after a long design dialogue, with each chunk approved in turn. The fuse-A3-into-A2 option was considered and **rejected** in favour of keeping A3 as a rewritten-plural agreement (lower blast radius, constellation stays 8 points).

**The five moves.** (1) A2 §2.7 makes stewardship the contribution-graded expression of membership, with the two Passport readings in §2.6; (2) A3 rewritten for plural co-stewards holding a collective *delega*, plus new §5A "the Hub's living balance"; (3) GTC GT-4 gains the settlement-over-time machinery — GT-4.3 weighting, GT-4.4A (heal-first / throttle / give / absorb / mutual aid), GT-4.6 the named Allocation Model, GT-4.8 graduated measured-balance intervention with the tighten-auto / loosen-deliberate asymmetry; (4) parameters land in **LGR-5** (lunation settlement, band of tolerance `n`, backstop `k`, upward-return terms); (5) Network Passport, A8 (Elder Council as common reservoir, new §4.2A), A7 (§4.6), Cheatsheet and Overview synced.

**Rationale.** The statute already commits the network to *tracking contribution and needs*; the homeostat is precisely a contribution-and-needs reconciliation engine (contribution = the give/top edge, needs = the absorb/bottom edge), and the design is the self-similar GT-4 allocation shape run two levels deeper (per-period, cross-period band, and the Council). Keeping **governance** (one member, one vote, at the Association) separate from **administration** (contribution-weighted *delega*, within holons) is what keeps the weighting compatible with CTS Art. 23 — the contested legal joint resolved by structure rather than drafting trick.

**One collision found and fixed.** GT-4.5(e) "no deficit passed forward" contradicted the new carried balance (GT-4.4A); reframed to distinguish cycle settlement (balance may move) from tenure handover (disclosed, reconciled, absorbed per GT-4.8(a)). A3 §5.3 (no personal cost guarantee) was checked and found *reinforced*, not contradicted.

**Alternatives considered and rejected.**
- **Fuse A3 into A2 (7-point constellation).** Rejected — cleaner conceptually but an ~18-file blast radius retiring a live binding agreement; the gradient is expressible across two documents without renumbering.
- **Dynamics-only (no contribution-weighting).** Held in reserve, not chosen — the owner directed the full model. Noted that the dynamics (homeostat/band/reservoir) and the franchise (weighting) are separable; the dynamics are legally safe, the weighting is the part most exposed to F1.
- **Embedding the math in the agreements.** Rejected — math lives in LGR-5; agreements carry guarantees + plain language, per the three-layer drafting approach.

**Open.** The relationship between **network-tier standing earned by feeding up** and the **Elder Council's elder-based composition** is left unresolved by design (flagged in A8 §4.2A and the strategy note).

## 2026-05-22 - Unified Fund architecture adopted

**Decision:** Adopt the Unified Fund as the network's single financial architecture, governed by one mathematical shape (a saturating cascade) applied at two scales — network and holon. The Association is the fiscal host for all holons on a public fiscal-hosting platform; each holon maintains its own collective; revenues and expenses pass through the Fund. The Network Regeneration Contribution (3–10% per GT-4.3, default 5%) is shaved at source on every GT-4 transaction. The cascade allocates the central pool through priority buckets — Holons, Stewards, Elder Council — with the Council holding allocation rights over the residual portion routed to it (Operating + Allocation buckets, per GT-4A.5). Stewards hold a *delega di spesa* on their holon's collective, shared with co-Stewards per the holon's own decision-making process. All flows are mission spending or activity-cost reimbursement; none constitutes personal income.

**Decided by:** vault owner, 2026-05-22, on the `unified-fund-draft-clauses` branch. The branch is the F1-review draft of the constellation clauses; the corresponding legal/fiscal review is the gating condition for merge.

**Rationale.** Many pots of money (NRF, Elder Fund, Care reserve, Floor Reserve) consolidated into one. The mathematical cascade gives soft priority protection without ring-fencing and self-scales to the Fund's actual balance. Targets in euros, not percentages, force honest projection against integral-regeneration priorities. The Elder Council's allocation rights operationalise long-form contribution as governance responsibility rather than personal reward — ETS-compliant by construction.

## 2026-05-17 - Reversal: tiers / passport / ledger collapsed to one access rule

**Decision:** Remove the four-tier Network Passport system (Seed/Sprout/Tree/Forest),
the role-scoped perk matrix, the blockchain/validator/dispute "Contribution Ledger"
apparatus, and the per-tier NRC discounts. Replace the entire participant-facing
mechanism with a single rule: **access to network resources is offered on a sliding

contributed, the higher your priority and the lower your price, up to free.** The
Contribution Ledger becomes a plain shared log; the Network Passport remains as a
portable cumulative record with no tiers. This reverses, in substance, GT-3A and the
tier/perk architecture built up over prior sessions.

**Decided by:** Holons (vault owner), 2026-05-17, via an explicit `/goal` directive
("simplify the whole passport / contribution ledger / tier to the simplest access
abundance / resource sharing system"), preceded by the owner's judgement that the

## 2026-05-10 — Membership becomes A2; Stewardship becomes A3

**Decision:** Renumber the constellation so that Membership is Agreement 2 and Stewardship is Agreement 3. New order: A1 Ownership → A2 Membership → A3 Stewardship → A4 Mentorship → A5 Sponsorship → A6 Partnership → A7 Intrapreneurship → A8 Eldership.

**Decided by:** Holons (vault owner) in session 2026-05-10. Executed by Claude under DataWizard protocol.

**Context.** Until this decision, Membership was Agreement 3 and Stewardship was Agreement 2. The Stewardship-before-Membership ordering had reflected an older architectural lens in which the operational layer (the steward who holds a hub) was foregrounded over the gateway layer (the member who joins). In practice — confirmed by Agreement 3's own Clause 1.4 ("Membership is a prerequisite for natural persons holding any active role in the ReGenerativa constellation") and by GT-11.4 — Membership is the gateway. No natural person can hold any other active agreement without first being a Member. The constellation's own logic placed Membership before Stewardship; the numbering did not.

**Rationale.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **Numbering should reflect the prerequisite chain.** Membership is the threshold every natural person crosses first. Stewardship is one of several roles that becomes available once that threshold has been crossed. A reader encountering the agreements in order should encounter the gateway before encountering the role.

2. **The Architecture of Belonging cascade reads more truly with the new order.** The Introduction's "From X comes Y" narrative arc — place → belonging → presence → expertise → support → collaboration → creation — is the deepening sequence the network actually follows. A person finds the place, chooses to belong, may then deepen into stewardship, etc. The previous numbering forced the cascade to invert at the second step (place → presence → belonging) which read as a logical jolt rather than a deepening.

3. **The numbering creates legibility for new readers.** A first-time reader of the constellation should be able to learn the architecture from the order itself. Membership (A2) before Stewardship (A3) makes the gateway prerequisite immediately visible. Stewardship (A2) before Membership (A3) required a separate explanation.

**Alternatives considered and rejected:**

- **Leaving the numbering as-is and adding more cross-references to clarify the prerequisite.** Rejected. The constellation already had several places (GT-11.4, A3 Clause 1.4, Cheatsheet "Prerequisite chain" line) explaining that Membership came before Stewardship logically. Adding more such notes would treat the symptom, not the cause. Numbering carries semantic weight; if the numbering misleads, the cure is to fix the numbering.

- **Inserting Membership as A0 or A1.5 to preserve all other numbers.** Rejected. Non-standard numbering creates more friction than it saves. A clean integer sequence from 1 to 8 is the most legible option for legal documents that will eventually be signed and filed by counterparties unfamiliar with the constellation's internal history.

- **Performing the swap in a future cleanup pass rather than now.** Rejected. The longer the inverted numbering persists, the more downstream artefacts (signed agreements, decision references, external citations) will calcify around it. None of those exist yet at scale; the cost of swapping is finite and bounded today.

**Consequences executed in the same session:**

- Two file renames (with intermediate temporary names to avoid filename collisions on Obsidian's filesystem)
- Two annex renames following the same pattern (A2-1 Steward Onboarding becomes A3-1 Steward Onboarding; A3-1 Member Onboarding becomes A2-1 Member Onboarding)
- Frontmatter `parent_agreement` and `title` updates on both renamed annexes
- Body cross-reference sweep across:
  - All 8 agreements (A2-A3 wikilinks, parenthetical "(Agreement N)" references in A8)
  - 4 affected annexes (the two renamed onboarding annexes, plus A1-3 Hub Care Protocol and A1-6 Hub DNA which reference Stewardship)
  - General Terms GT-11.3 constellation list (reordered)
  - Constellation Cheatsheet (Eight Agreements table, Prerequisite chain note, Key Numbers table, Annexes-at-a-Glance table)
  - Introduction narrative (Architecture of Belonging cascade rewritten in new order; "Each part sustains the whole" sentences rewritten to match)
