# Radical Simplification - One Rule

*Proposal only. This document changes nothing in the vault. It exists so the simplified
shape can be seen whole, on one page, before any decision to execute. Triggered by the
owner's judgment (2026-05-16) that the contribution-to-access mechanism has accreted
accidental complexity. Companion to [[Digital Infrastructure Gap Review - 2026-05-16]]
and [[Constellation Viability Review - 2026-05-16]].*

---

## The whole participant-facing system, in one rule

> **Access to the network's resources is priced on a sliding scale from full price to
> free, set by each person's standing contribution. The more you have contributed, the
> higher your booking priority and the lower your price - up to free.**

One continuous number per person. One consequence. No tiers, no passport, no ceremony.

---

## How contribution is measured (kept deliberately cheap)

- **One number: Contribution Standing.** Continuous and cumulative. Harvest's existing
  `scoring` already computes exactly this (a weighted sum of logged activity) - no new
  system is required.
- **Validated by judgment, not machinery.** A steward or a peer confirms a logged
  contribution; a plain, visible log. No blockchain, no validator roles, no dispute
  thresholds. The network is small; fuzziness is acceptable and far cheaper than the
  apparatus built to deny it.
- **Stewards set the dial per resource.** "Top few free, then sliding to full price" -
  decided per programme or hub stay by the people running it, not by a global tier
  table.

---

## What this deletes (accidental complexity)

| Deleted | Why it was accidental |
|---|---|
| Seed / Sprout / Tree / Forest tiers | A discretisation of a number that is already continuous. Adds a status narrative, no mechanism. |
| Network Passport ceremony + four-tier perk matrix | The perk matrix is just "more contribution = more access" enumerated by hand, role by role. |
| LGR-3 (tier thresholds) | Exists only to draw lines on the continuous number. Gone with the tiers. |
| Contribution Ledger as blockchain / validators / disputes (the A6-4 apparatus) | Solved a trust problem the network's scale does not yet have. Replaced by log + judgment. |
| Per-tier NRC discounts | Pricing already flows from the one rule; the tier carve-outs were redundant. |

This proposal **deletes more than it adds**. That is the point.

---

## What stays (essential - not the same complexity)

- **The eight role agreements.** An owner lending a building, a steward running it, a
  mentor teaching, a partner co-selling are genuinely different legal relationships
  under Italian Third Sector law. They stay - but each sheds its tier/perk annex
  language and points to the one rule.
- **The legal / fiscal skeleton** (comodato, the Regeneration Contribution to the Fund,
  bilancio sociale). Legal reality, not design bloat. Simplification must not entangle
  the perks logic back into it.

---

## Eldership: decoupled, not deleted by stealth

"Who guards the mission, and what survives if the Association dissolves" is a different
problem that only looked unified because Forest = top tier = Elder. Two honest options,
to be decided on their own merits:

- **(a) Drop the Eldership layer.** Continuity becomes the Statute's and Board's job
  (GT-13), explicitly.
- **(b) Keep a tiny, separate continuity circle.** Long-serving stewards may be invited
  to a named group holding the GT-13.5 custodial role - not the summit of an access
  ladder.

This proposal does not pick. It only insists the choice is made deliberately, not
inherited from the tier ladder.

---

## The honest cost of simplifying (so this is a real decision, not a sales pitch)

- **You lose the status narrative.** Seed-to-Tree is genuinely motivating for some
  communities; a bare number is not a story. A trade-off, not a free win.
- **A single scalar flattens kinds of contribution.** A quiet year of land-tending and
  a visible workshop collapse to one figure. The tier system did not actually fix this
  either - steward judgment in the loop is the real answer in both the simple and the
  complex version.
- **Continuity must be consciously rehoused.** If Eldership is dropped, GT-13 and the
  Statute must genuinely carry dissolution-resilience, not merely be assumed to.
