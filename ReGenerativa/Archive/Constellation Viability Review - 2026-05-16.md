# Constellation Viability Review - 2026-05-16

*Pre-signing audit of the full Constellation of Agreements for clarity, balance
(reciprocity), and viability (legal/structural coherence). Actionable decisions are
tracked in [[0.5 Open Questions]]. Scope per owner decision 2026-05-16: all eight
agreements must be sign-ready.*

---

## Headline verdict

The architecture is **sound**. The constellation is clear, balanced, and largely
viable. Nothing found is a design flaw. What stands between it and "all eight
sign-ready" is **completion of the two draft agreements and two undefined numbers**,
not structural defects.

- **Clarity:** strong. Tier naming (Seed/Sprout/Tree/Forest) consistent everywhere;
  annexes 18/18 contiguous; no stale wikilinks; no agreement-to-Operational-Procedures
  timeframe contradictions. One real gap: the Introduction.
- **Balance:** holds across all eight agreements. Every role gives and gets. The
  Association's former soft-obligations were already hardened in A1/A4/A7. A5
  Sponsorship and A8 Eldership are asymmetric *by design*, each with an explicit
  non-extraction protection.
- **Viability:** the legal hierarchy (Statute > agreement > GT), Italian-law citations
  (comodato d'uso, D.Lgs 117/2017, TUIR), the Membership-gateway prerequisite chain,
  and the GT-13 dissolution-continuity protocol are internally coherent.

---

## Method and a correction to the record

Three parallel read-only audits covered (1) the foundation layer (GT, Introduction,
Overview, Cheatsheet), (2) the eight agreement bodies, and (3) the 18 annexes plus four
companion docs. Every consequential or conflicting claim was then verified directly
against the source files.

**One sub-audit materially overstated severity** and is corrected here: it reported
A7-2 (Phase Review), A7-3 (Graduation), and A8-1 (Elder Council Charter) as "missing /
not created" and graded them BLOCKERS. They **exist** - confirmed against the directory
listing, the MOC, and the files themselves. That sub-audit had only the agreement
bodies in scope and inferred absence from cross-references it could not see. The genuine
residue is narrower: A8's own Linked Annexes section still labels A8-1 "(to be
created)" though A8-1 is a drafted v1.0, and A8-2 (Transition Rite Record) is the only
annex genuinely not yet created.
