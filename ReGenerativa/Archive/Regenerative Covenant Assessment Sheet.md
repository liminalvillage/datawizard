# Regenerative Covenant - Seasonal Assessment Sheet

*A fillable instrument for the seasonal Regenerative Impact review. It operationalises the one-page seasonal format in the [[Regenerative Impact Framework]] (RIF, GT-1.6), reading a Hub's standing against the six dimensions of the Regenerative Covenant ([[General Terms and Conditions|GT-2]]). One sheet per Hub per season (equinox / solstice, or by lunation). Its output feeds the Hub's regenerative balance (GT-2.7), the Hub DNA living-progress table, and the network's annual synthesis in the bilancio sociale. Built to run side-by-side with a Hub's [[Integral Quadrants of the Constellation - 2026-06-27|8 Forms of Capital]] abundance sheet - see the crosswalk at the foot.*

---

## Principles before you score

Read these first. They govern how the numbers are used.

- **Honest attention, not a grade.** The point is to see truthfully, not to perform well. A page of honest "↓ and here's why" is worth more than a wall of 5s.
- **Trend over level.** Direction of travel is the primary reading. A hub at "2 and rising" is healthier than one at "4 and falling."
- **Non-attainment is never a fault** (GT-2.4). The covenant is a shared commitment, not a personal liability. A hard season honestly met is never penalised (GT-2.7(b)).
- **Not a ranking.** Each Hub rates only *itself*, against *its own* targets (Annex 2 §D), never against another Hub. The optional 1-5 columns are a private reflective aid, not a cross-hub score.
- **Numbers serve sensing.** Where a score and a felt sense disagree, the sense is data too. Metrics are the servant (see the perspective check below).
- **The sheet initiates, it never executes.** A persistent decline opens a *supportive review* (GT-2.7), beginning with a conversation. It never of itself removes a Steward or a Hub.

## The balance check across perspectives
*The proposed RIF v1.5 addition, piloted here. The six dimensions can also be read by perspective. The outer perspective is the easiest to measure and so the easiest to over-weight.*

| Perspective | What it holds | Covenant dimensions | Attended this season? | Over- or under-weighted? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inner (I)** | dignity, meaning, rest | B-interior | | |
| **Between (We)** | trust, culture, belonging | E, C, F-integrity | | |
| **Outer (Its)** | land, economy, infrastructure | A, D, F-structure, B-physical | | |

**The one balancing question:** *Are we attending to all three - inner, between, and outer - or only the measurable one?* Where the honest answer is "only the measurable," name the lagging perspective as what needs attention, and let a **relational or sensing practice** (a reflection round, a conversation, a sensing circle) be the response - not a new metric.

> A soil test is not ecological relationship. A contribution score is not belonging. A cost-coverage figure is not dignity.

## From assessment to response (GT-2.7)

1. **Any dimension persistently ↓ across multiple seasons, or any active harm?** ☐ no ☐ yes → opens a **supportive review** (GT-2.7): re-tune targets to real carrying capacity; direct mentorship and attention to the lagging dimension; mutual aid from the common reservoir where cost is the constraint (GT-4.8(a)). Support first.
2. **Earned autonomy.** A Hub regenerating well over seasons earns a wider band, lighter-touch review, and Passport recognition (GT-2.7(e)).
3. **Lead and lag.** Which *one* dimension, if it shifted, would move the most others? "Neglect of any one will eventually undermine the others" (RIF). Name the lever, not a to-do list.

## Crosswalk to the 8 Forms of Capital

| 8 Forms of Capital | Covenant dimension | Perspective |
|---|---|---|
| Financial | D | Outer |
| Material | D (+ A built) | Outer |
| Living / Natural | A | Outer |
| Social | E | Between |
| Cultural | C + E | Between |
| Intellectual | C | Between / Outer |
| Experiential | B | Inner / Outer |
| Spiritual | B-interior | Inner |
| *(no 8FoC equivalent)* | **F - Governance & Integrity** | Between / Outer |

*Two reciprocal gaps to hold: the 8 capitals carry no Governance / Integrity form (covenant F supplies it); the covenant has no explicit Spiritual dimension (it folds into B-interior - the inner perspective the sheet's balance check is built to protect).*
