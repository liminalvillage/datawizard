# ReGenerativa — Project Guidelines

*Read this file first. It is the agent's orientation document for this project.*

---

## 1. Project Identity

**ReGenerativa APS — ETS** is an Italian Third Sector Association (Associazione di Promozione Sociale / Ente del Terzo Settore), registered in Castorano (AP) on 21 December 2024, tax code 92074380442, inscribed in the RUNTS.

Its purpose is to build and govern a **network of regenerative hubs** — physical places managed through a holonic architecture of agreements, digital infrastructure, and shared governance. Each hub is a node in a syntropic system designed to increase vitality, coherence, and mutual benefit without extracting value from any part.

**Governed by:** Italian law, Legislative Decree 3 July 2017, No. 117 (Third Sector Code).

## 3. Key Concepts (quick reference)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Constellation** | The six interlocking agreements that govern all roles in the network |
| **Hub** | A physical regenerative place within the network |
| **Hub DNA** | Values, norms, rhythms, and use conditions specific to a hub |
| **Social DNA Framework** | Six-domain impact classification: Environment & Natural Systems, Health & Well-Being, Education & Innovation, Economy & Infrastructure, Community & Culture, Governance & Ethical Values |
| **Contribution Ledger** | Digital/blockchain system tracking validated contributions |
| **Network Regeneration Fund** | Common fund receiving ≥5% of net revenue from economic activities |
| **Holon** | An entity that is simultaneously self-contained and part of a larger whole — the design principle underlying the whole architecture |
| **Syntropic system** | A system that increases vitality and mutual benefit rather than extracting from one part to benefit another |
| **Comodato d'uso** | Italian civil law gratuitous loan for use (Art. 1803–1812 Civil Code) — the legal basis for the Asset Use Agreement |

## 4. The Six Agreements (Constellation Structure)

| Agreement             | Layer         | Role                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ownership Agreement   | Root          | Owner grants gratuitous purpose-bound use; retains ownership          |
| Stewardship Agreement | Operational   | Delegated custodian manages hub day-to-day                            |
| Membership Agreement  | Relational    | Members circulate through hubs as contributors/beneficiaries          |
| Mentorship Agreement  | Expertise     | Expert practitioners host programmes; tiered access model             |
| Sponsorship Agreement | Support       | Benefactors channel resources without acquiring control               |
| Partnership Agreement | Collaboration | Multi-entity commercial projects with contribution-based distribution |

All agreements incorporate the General Terms and Conditions. The Statute prevails over all.

## 9. Notes for Agents

- The Constellation v3 source file in `Sources/` is the primary reference document. Check `harvest_status` before processing it.
- When iterating on agreement text, edit the individual agreement files in `Agreements/` — not the source file.
- The General Terms apply to all agreements — changes there ripple everywhere. Flag any GT edits for explicit human review before writing.
- Italian legal terminology should be preserved precisely. Do not paraphrase clauses in ways that alter legal meaning.
- When uncertain about legal intent, ask rather than interpret.
