*Read this file first. It is the agent's orientation document for this project.*

## 1. Project Identity

**ReGenerativa APS — ETS** is an Italian Third Sector Association (Associazione di Promozione Sociale / Ente del Terzo Settore), registered in Castorano (AP) on 21 December 2024, tax code 92074380442, inscribed in the RUNTS.

Its purpose is to build and govern a **network of regenerative hubs** — physical places managed through a holonic architecture of agreements, digital infrastructure, and shared governance. Each hub is a node in a syntropic system designed to increase vitality, coherence, and mutual benefit without extracting value from any part.

**Governed by:** Italian law, Legislative Decree 3 July 2017, No. 117 (Third Sector Code).

## 3. Key Concepts (quick reference)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Constellation** | The eight interlocking agreements that govern all roles in the network |
| **Hub** | A physical regenerative place within the network |
| **Hub DNA** | Values, norms, rhythms, and use conditions specific to a hub |
| **Regenerative Impact Framework** | Six-dimension impact classification per GT-1.6: (a) Land and Ecological Systems, (b) Human Health and Well-Being, (c) Knowledge and Cultural Vitality, (d) Economic Fairness and Material Infrastructure, (e) Community and Relational Culture, (f) Governance and Ethical Integrity |
| **Contribution Ledger** | A simple, shared, visible log of validated contributions (a plain record, not a blockchain) |
| **Network Passport** | Portable, cumulative contribution record per participant; no tiers — drives one sliding-scale access rule (more contribution → higher priority and lower price, up to free) |
| **Unified Fund** | The Association's single financial architecture (GT-1.10) — fiscal host for all holons (Association holon + Hub/Project/Venture/Elder Council holons); every holon runs the same self-similar allocation shape per GT-4 (NRC out → cost-coverage → discretionary → upward overflow to Elder Council) |
| **Allocation model (one shape, every holon)** | The single self-similar loop applied at every level (transaction, hub, project, venture, Elder Council, network): **NRC** off the top → cover **cost target `C`** → hold **allocation right** over the surplus *within the band* (up to edge `nC`), directed by the holon's own **value system** → past `nC`, overflow rises to the **Elder Council / common reservoir** (terminal), which also covers holons that fall below floor. Smooth fill-then-spill via a `tanh` curve; settles each **lunation** on a carried balance `B`. GT-4.4 / 4.4A / 4.6 / 4.8; parameters in LGR-5 *(pending F1)* |
| **NRC (Network Regeneration Contribution)** | The off-the-top network fee on any recorded transaction; Board-set, uniform, 3–10% (default 5%). GT-4.4(a) |
| **Common reservoir** | The shared pool at the Elder Council that healthy holons overflow into and struggling holons draw from — mutual insurance, not charity (formerly "mutual-aid reservoir"). GT-4.4A, GT-4.8 |
| **Value system** | Each holon's own declared weighting of contribution indicators (egalitarian default / hours / roles / mix) that converts its Contribution Ledger into allocation-right shares. GT-4.3A |
| **Network Demand Register** | Living record of unmet network needs that any member can respond to (the basis of intrapreneurship) |
| **Holon** | An entity that is simultaneously self-contained and part of a larger whole — the design principle underlying the whole architecture |
| **Syntropic system** | A system that increases vitality and mutual benefit rather than extracting from one part to benefit another |
| **Comodato d'uso** | Italian civil law gratuitous loan for use (Art. 1803–1812 Civil Code) — the legal basis for the Ownership Agreement |

## 4. The Eight Agreements (Constellation Structure)

| #   | Agreement                | Layer         | Role                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Ownership Agreement      | Root          | Owner grants gratuitous purpose-bound use; retains ownership                               |
| 2   | Membership Agreement     | Gateway       | Members enter the democratic life of the Association; prerequisite for all other roles    |
| 3   | Stewardship Agreement    | Operational   | Delegated custodian manages hub day-to-day                                                 |
| 4   | Mentorship Agreement     | Expertise     | Expert practitioners host programmes; gift-economy access for highest contributors        |
| 5   | Sponsorship Agreement    | Support       | Benefactors channel resources without acquiring control                                    |
| 6   | Partnership Agreement    | Collaboration | Multi-entity commercial projects with contribution-based distribution                      |
| 7   | Intrapreneurship Agreement | Generative  | Venture Holons activated in response to documented Network Demand Signals                  |
| 8   | Eldership Agreement      | Wisdom        | Long-term contributors transition into governance and witness role; Elder Council         |

All agreements incorporate the General Terms and Conditions. The Statute prevails over all.

## 9. Notes for Agents

- Each agreement is a stable instrument once signed. Edits to agreement text are deliberate architectural changes — show proposed edits in plain text first, get explicit approval, then write.
- The General Terms apply to all agreements — changes there ripple everywhere. Flag any GT edits for explicit human review before writing.
- Italian legal terminology should be preserved precisely. Do not paraphrase clauses in ways that alter legal meaning.
- The Operational Procedures companion doc holds operational timeframes that the network can tune by Board resolution; substantive rights live in the agreements themselves.
- When uncertain about legal intent or architectural placement, ask rather than interpret.
