# Capital Architecture - Funding the Path to Commons

*This note resolves SG6 of [[Constellation Structural Gaps - 2026-06-16]] the light way: it records the decided capital architecture and the owner's choices, and deliberately defers the clause work until the network has an acquisition actually in view. Nothing here amends the agreements. All of it is F1-relevant; the fiscal questions are in [[F1 Nota per il Commercialista - 2026-06-13]] (domanda 12).*

---

## The gap (SG6, restated)

The path-to-commons (A1 §6.3A; [[Regeneration Annex - Value Return on Sale - 2026-06-11]] §9) is under-funded by construction: the Asset Reserve (GT-4.4B) is per-asset and fills slowly; donations are opportunistic; A1 §6.3A(c)'s "topped up where necessary by Board allocation" names no actual source; and the common reservoir is expressly for operating shortfalls, not capital (GT-4.8). A €100k+ real-property acquisition has no funding instrument.

## The frame: the fund architecture is a 2×2

Local/shared × operating/capital:

| | Operating | Capital |
|---|---|---|
| **Local (per holon)** | Carried balance `B` (GT-4.4A) | Asset Reserve (GT-4.4B) |
| **Shared (network)** | Common reservoir (GT-4.8) | **— the missing quadrant** |

Three quadrants exist. SG6 is precisely the fourth: shared capital.

## What was decided (owner Q&A, RG S42)

1. **The per-asset Asset Reserve stays.** It is load-bearing: it is the landing spot for asset-generated value (the ARC, GT-4.9; regenerated value `V`, Regeneration Annex §9.5); it embodies "local value stays local" in capital form (GT-4.4B(d)); it covers ordinary replacement (the van, the workshop kit) as the *riserva vincolata* a comodato asset needs in place of depreciation (F1 Nota Q9); its protection is structural (position in the shape, GT-4.4B(a)); it ports with the holon when a comodato ends; and it is inert (`R = 0`) where there is nothing to replace. Collapsing it into a central pot would trade three real properties for one fewer clause.

2. **The shared-capital quadrant is known, named, and deferred.** Direction when it is built: a network-level capital pool — preferably a **Commons Acquisition Fund holon** running the standard GT-4.4 shape, earmarked to acquisition, distinct from the operating reservoir (no cross-subsidy in either direction) — that tops up an asset's reserve when an acquisition window opens. **It is not built now**: with three hubs and no live acquisition, a fund holon + GT clause + LGR would be architecture ahead of reality, and it is F1-gated anyway.

3. **Acceptable instruments (all four, when live):** grants and donations/legacies earmarked to acquisition (Agreement 5); community/solidarity shares (fits ReFactory's solidarity-economy framing); crowdfunding/campaigns for a named acquisition; and mission-aligned debt.

4. **Debt appetite: moderate.** Borrowing for acquisition by Board decision within published (future-LGR) limits — against an identified repayment source, and never secured on a comodato Asset without the Owner's written consent. Debt serviced from capital inflows, never from the operating reservoir.

5. **Capital confers no rights.** Whatever instrument is used: no governance vote, no property right (GT-11.5), no personal profit (GT-4.5(g)); the money→access firewall (GT-3A.4A) holds.

## Deliberately left untouched

- **A1 §6.3A(c)** keeps its "topped up where necessary by Board allocation" wording until the Fund exists to point at. Editing a signed agreement to reference an unbuilt instrument would be premature.
- **No GT-4.4D, no LGR-10, no fund holon** until an acquisition is in view. The trigger to build: a concrete acquisition candidate (a §6.3A window opening, an Owner signalling disposal, or a donation-with-costs) or the F1 answers landing, whichever first.

## F1 dependencies (asked as domanda 12 in the Nota)

(a) ETS borrowing capacity and security — including ipoteca on owned vs comodato property (links Nota Q7(b)); (b) the admissible legal form of community/solidarity shares for an APS (social lending? titoli di solidarietà via partner bank? equity is excluded by non-distribution); (c) fiscal treatment of acquisition-earmarked crowdfunding (liberalità vs corrispettivo); (d) a future acquisition pool as *riserva vincolata* at network level (extends Nota Q9 from asset to network scale); (e) whether a large capital raise disturbs the Art. 79(5) commercial-prevalence test (links Nota Q5).
