# Regeneration Annex - Value Return on Sale

*Design note, 2026-06-11. Owner-approved architecture; clause drafting not yet started; everything here is (Pending F1) territory with the comodato-modale question as F1's headline item. Origin: the stewardship balance analysis of 2026-06-10/11, which identified the one structural extraction the constellation does not yet address.*

---

## 1. The problem

The network's regenerative labour and money capitalise into the Owner's private asset. When the Owner sells, the Owner captures that uplift alone. Under the syntropic principle (no value extracted from any part - 0.0 §1), this is the deepest unresolved imbalance in the constellation: the ruin the network rebuilds over five years becomes a jewel, and the sale of the jewel owes the network nothing. A1 §3.4/Annex 4 settle *unamortised investment* on early termination; nothing settles *created value* on sale.

The protection-gradient finding of the [[Constellation Balance Review - 2026-06-10]] sharpens it: the classes who create the value (resident stewards above all) have the thinnest protection, while the Owner class holds both the asset and the appreciation.

## 2. Options considered and rejected

| Option | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| **Steward-personal right of first refusal** | Stewards rotate and rarely have capital to match a buyer's price in 60 days; a right they cannot exercise. Contractual prelazione also has obligatory effect only (damages, not recovery). |
| **Sale-uplift paid to stewards in euros, prorata** | Three walls: (a) money routed via the Association to member-stewards risks indirect profit distribution, prohibited for an APS/ETS (Art. 8, d.lgs. 117/2017) - can cost RUNTS status; (b) reverses the constellation's money-out-of-personal-returns firewalls (GT-11.5, GT-4.8(d), GT-3A.4/4A) and tilts steward incentives from regenerative use toward property-value maximisation; (c) prorata allocation across rotating stewards in euros is the dispute class the constellation exists to avoid. |
| **Voluntary covenant (opt-in annex)** | Adverse selection: signed precisely by the owners who would have returned the value anyway, skipped by the ones it exists for. |
| **Recognition-only return (Eldership track)** | Recognition works on people who want to stay in relationship; a seller is by definition leaving. No teeth. Retained as a rider (§7 below), not as the engine. |
| **Standalone side contract** | Legally workable (keeps A1 a pure comodato) but adds a second instrument to negotiate and link. Superseded by in-A1 placement with a conversion clause (§4) - worst case, the law itself degrades the clause into the side contract, so nothing is lost by drafting it inside A1. |

## 3. The decided architecture: investment-gated onboarding, binary

Two states for any asset. The trigger is the network's own spending decision, reusing the gate that already exists (A1 §3.4: no above-threshold spend until Annex 4 is completed).

**Lent as-is.** Standard A1. No baseline, no value-return clause, no encumbrance. Network spend stays below the Annex 4 threshold under the §3.4 defaults. Most tools, vehicles, usable spaces.

**Regeneration onboarding.** The moment *planned* investment crosses the threshold - before the first euro is spent - one instrument is signed: the **Regeneration Annex** (the natural evolution of Annex 4), in a single notarial sitting where real property is involved. Contents:

1. **Baseline** - asset value and OMI zone reference recorded at signing. The arithmetic anchor for everything downstream; recording it on day one is what prevents the end-of-relationship valuation fight.
2. **Investment schedule with amortisation** - what §3.4/Annex 4 already require today.
3. **Regeneration payout (value return on sale)** - if the Owner sells or transfers the asset for value during the term **or within five (5) years after termination**, the Owner pays the Association an agreed share of the **Regenerative Premium** = sale price - (baseline x OMI zone appreciation index). The post-exit clawback window tapers (minus 20% per year) so the obligation fades as the network's imprint does; without it the dodge is trivial (terminate, wait, sell clean).
4. **ROFR to the Association at formula price** (baseline + market index, premium excluded). The Owner's choice at sale: pay the network its share, or sell the asset to the network without it. Either way the network's value comes home. 90-day exercise window; notice mechanics mirror A1 §6.3.
5. **Payment-at-closing mechanics + clausola penale** (Art. 1382 c.c.) for selling without settling.
6. **Ipoteca volontaria** (real property only) - separate notarial deed, same sitting. See §5.

Mid-life re-tiering rides the existing §3.4 mechanism: if unplanned investment later crosses the threshold, the Annex 4 update is where the Regeneration Annex gets signed before another euro is spent. Self-enforcing; no policing.

Money flows **Owner → Association / common reservoir** - never to stewards personally (Art. 8). Stewards are made whole through the reservoir their asset has first call on (GT-4.8(a)), and through the recognition machinery (§7).

## 4. Legal placement: inside A1, framed as restitution

Comodato is gratuitous by definition (Art. 1803 c.c.); a payment obligation drafted as consideration for use risks recharacterising the root agreement (registration tax, lease-law consequences). The drafting strategy:

- **Extend the existing causa, do not invent a new one.** A1 §3.4 already obliges the Owner to settle unamortised network investment on early termination without anyone calling the comodato onerous. The value-return clause is the same animal extended from "early termination" to "sale": settlement of value the network's documented investment and labour created - restitution / prevention of unjust enrichment, not price.
- **State gratuitousness expressly.** The clause opens by confirming the use remains gratuitous and that the provision settles network-created value only.
- **Anchor the formula in the documented record.** Basis: the network's logged investment and labour (A1-6 Asset Care Protocol maintenance log + the asset's Ledger partition). The OMI-indexed premium acts as measure and cap. This strengthens the restitution framing and makes the number computable instead of arguable.
- **Conversion clause** (Art. 1424 c.c. pattern): if the provision is ever held incompatible with the comodato's gratuitous nature, it survives as an autonomous agreement between the Parties on the same terms - graceful degradation into the side-contract form.
- Italian doctrine tolerates burdens in a comodato (*comodato modale*) so long as the burden is not effectively the price of use; a contingent, sale-triggered, investment-anchored settlement is about as far from rent as a payment can be. **F1 must vet this; it is the headline question.**

## 5. The ipoteca: when, why, how much

**When.** Only where the asset is real property and the Regeneration Annex is being signed (i.e. above-threshold investment). Movables get the package minus the mortgage; their premiums are trivial. The threshold figure should live in an LGR (the constellation's pattern for tunable numbers), set high enough that the notarial cost stays proportionate to the spend it secures.

**Why it is the only instrument with teeth.** (a) An unsecured §6.3A claim is personal - the buyer takes the property free, and the remedy is a lawsuit against someone who may have neither asset nor money. (b) A registered ipoteca appears in the visura ipotecaria every buyer's notary runs; no purchase closes with an undischarged mortgage on title, so the notary withholds the payoff and the Association releases the registration against payment (assenso alla cancellazione). Promise-to-pay becomes structurally-impossible-to-evade. (c) Secured priority if the Owner has other creditors.

**For how much.** Art. 2809 c.c. (specialità): a mortgage registers for a fixed maximum sum - "X% of a future premium" cannot be registered. So the registration is a **ceiling**, and the claim settles within it. Recommended: **cap = investment schedule x 150-200%** (network labour compounds beyond euros spent), revised upward by supplementary registration at each Annex 4 schedule update. Alternative: flat 15-25% of baseline value. Worked example: EUR 300k farmhouse, EUR 30k planned investment → cap ~EUR 60k; imposta ipotecaria 2% (~EUR 1,200) + notary ≈ EUR 2.5-3.5k all-in, borne by the network and logged as network investment in the asset's Ledger partition. Registration lasts 20 years, renewable.

**Known gap.** If the premium vastly outruns the registered cap (ruin-becomes-jewel), the excess above the ceiling is unsecured. The cap-revision-at-each-schedule-update mechanism keeps the gap small; review it whenever Annex 4 is touched.

## 7. Recognition rider: the Owner's Eldership track

Retained from the design conversation as garnish, not engine. A natural-person Owner's premium settlement (or premium-free sale to the Association under the ROFR) is recorded in their Passport as a Validated Contribution event **amount-blind** - naming the act, the asset, and the years under covenant, never the euros (GT-3A.4/4A firewalls intact; no access-priority effect). Years holding A1 already count toward LGR-1's five cumulative years; the settlement becomes a named, weighty fact in the "long and substantial contribution record" A8 §2.1 nominators judge. Standard disclaimers mirror A8 §4.2A: no vote, no seat, no allocation voice. Entity Owners: recognition at entity level (supporter directory / named legacy), no Eldership path (A8 §2.1 natural persons only). This completes a lifecycle the constellation lacks: **Owner → Elder** - selling becomes a possible transition into the network rather than an exit from it.

## 8. F1 questions (headline items for the legal/fiscal review)

1. **Comodato modale limits** - does the sale-triggered, investment-anchored value-return clause stay within the burdens a gratuitous comodato tolerates, with the restitution framing and conversion clause as drafted? Consequences if recharacterised (registration tax, lease rules)?
2. **Ipoteca for an ETS** - tax treatment of a voluntary mortgage in favour of an APS/ETS: does the general 2% imposta ipotecaria apply or is there Third-Sector relief? Practical notarial mechanics of cap revision by supplementary registration?
3. **Prelazione agraria interaction** - where a steward personally cultivates farmland as coltivatore diretto/IAP, statutory agricultural pre-emption (L. 590/1965, L. 817/1971) may apply with real (retratto) effect regardless of the Annex. Map the interaction with the Association's contractual ROFR.
4. **Art. 8 CTS confirmation** - confirm the Owner → Association premium flow is a liberal donation with no indirect-distribution exposure, and check deductibility (Art. 83 CTS).
5. **Clawback enforceability** - the 5-year post-termination tapering obligation: enforceable as drafted, or does it need its own security/registration to survive termination of A1?
6. **OMI baseline mechanics** - is the OMI zone index sufficiently determinate for a formula clause, or should the Annex name a fallback (e.g. a jointly appointed valuer) where OMI granularity fails?
