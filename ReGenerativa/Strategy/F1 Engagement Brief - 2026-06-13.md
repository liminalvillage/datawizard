# F1 Engagement Brief

*The consolidated agenda for the formal F1 legal/fiscal review — everything accumulated against the gate since March 2026, sorted by advisor and priority. Supersedes the shorter agenda list at the foot of [[F1 Pre-Review - Fiscal Notes - 2026-06-10]] (which remains the fiscal pre-analysis of record). ## What F1 is

The formal engagement of two professionals **before anything is signed**: a **commercialista** versed in Third Sector (ETS) fiscal rules, and a **Third Sector lawyer** for the contract-law side (possibly one firm covering both). Every clause drafted since March 2026 is an unsigned draft carrying *(Pending F1)* — currently ~96 markers across 15 files. F1 is the ratification gate: the advisors opine, the drafts are amended per their advice, the markers clear, signing begins.

The 2026-06-10 internal fiscal pre-review ([[F1 Pre-Review - Fiscal Notes - 2026-06-10]]) arms this engagement; it explicitly does not substitute for it. It was reasoned from the CTS and general practice **without** the Statute or current Agenzia delle Entrate circulars — both must be in the advisors' hands.

## What is at stake

Four exposures, each potentially existential for the APS-ETS status:

1. **Becoming a commercial entity by accident** (Art. 79(5) CTS) — if commercial revenue (external NRC, market-rate bands, partnership flows) comes to prevail.
2. **Donations reclassified as purchases** — a donation earning the donor a concrete benefit loses *spirito di liberalità*: donor loses Art. 83 relief, the receipt becomes taxable, VAT-able commercial revenue. GT-3A.4A was built against this.
3. **Paying volunteers** (Art. 17(3) CTS) — volunteer work may not be remunerated in any way; the sliding-scale access rule converts (largely volunteer) contribution into economic benefit. The biggest cross-cutting question.
4. **Indirect profit distribution** (Art. 8(2) CTS) — any flow working out to a return to a member breaches the non-distribution constraint that defines an ETS.
