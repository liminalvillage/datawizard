# Knowledge Commons

*Design note, 2026-06-20. The rationale behind **GT-2.9** and `ReGenerativa/LICENSE.md`. Addresses Structural Gap **SG9** ([[Constellation Structural Gaps - 2026-06-16]]).*

---

## The stance

The constellation is itself valuable intellectual work — and the network's ethos is commons-first and openly anti-extractive ("the victory of the commons"; the hubs brand as open-source). It would be incoherent to hold the *model for building a commons* as private property. So the model is **released to the commons under CC BY-SA 4.0** (Attribution-ShareAlike): free to use and adapt, attribution required, derivatives shared alike — open, reciprocal, and self-perpetuating.

## Why CC BY-SA (and not the alternatives)

- **vs CC0 / public domain** — share-alike keeps derivatives open and lets improvements return to the commons; pure public domain allows enclosure of forks.
- **vs CC BY (attribution only)** — same, no copyleft; rejected for the same reason (a fork could be closed).
- **vs a copyfair / Peer Production License** (free for commoners, paid for for-profit extractors) — the most ideologically aligned with the syntropic no-extraction principle, but non-standard, less legally tested, and not "open" in the recognised sense; CC BY-SA was chosen for legibility and movement-standard reciprocity. *(Revisitable: a copyfair stance remains an option if extraction by for-profit adopters becomes a real concern.)*

## Scope

Covered: the agreements, General Terms, companion frameworks (RIF), and templates (Hub DNA, Operational Procedures, Network Passport, etc.). **Not** covered: members'/participants' personal data (GT-5); hub- or owner-private information; and Venture IP, which Agreement 7 §9 governs on its own terms.

## Note on share-alike for legal templates
Share-alike means an adopter who adapts these agreements into their own governance must license *their* version under CC BY-SA too. This is intended — it keeps the governance commons open — but it is the one point of friction for adopters who want closed derivatives; flagged for F1 awareness, not as a blocker.

## Relationship
GT-2.9 is the binding declaration; `ReGenerativa/LICENSE.md` is the concrete artifact; this note is the rationale. Sharing the model is the engine of federation (GT-4.10) and movement growth. Venture IP stays separate (A7 §9).
