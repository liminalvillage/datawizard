# F1 Pre-Review - Fiscal Notes

*An internal pre-review of the constellation's fiscally sensitive mechanics from a commercialista / tax-advisor perspective, prepared to arm the formal F1 legal/fiscal review - not to substitute for it. Reasoned from the Third Sector Code (D.Lgs 117/2017, "CTS") and general Italian practice, not from the Statute's text or current Agenzia delle Entrate circulars; the F1 advisor's opinion governs. The four reviewed items came from the F1-FISCAL tier of [[Developmental Altitude of the Constellation - 2026-06-06]]; three were applied 2026-06-10 on the strength of this review (see [[0.3 Decision Log]]), one was dropped.*

---

## Headline

The four F1-tier items were not fiscally equal. One (the money-to-access firewall) was not merely safe - the *prior* design created the tax exposure, making the fix near-mandatory. One (flex NRC) was advised against and dropped. Two were fine with conditions, now baked into the clauses. The review also surfaced one cross-cutting issue larger than any single item - the Art. 17(3) CTS volunteer-remuneration question - which the F1 advisor must opine on explicitly.

---

## Item 1 - Money-to-access firewall (applied as GT-3A.4A, full exclusion)

The previous design let support under Agreement 5 be logged as a Validated Contribution feeding the contribution score that sets Programme claim priority and sliding-scale prices. Exposure:

1. **Reclassification of the donation.** An *erogazione liberale* requires *spirito di liberalità* - no counter-performance. A payment earning a score that yields concrete economic benefits (priority access, free places) can be recharacterised as a **corrispettivo**. The donor loses Art. 83 CTS benefits (30% detrazione IRPEF or deduction up to 10% of income); the Association's receipt becomes commercial revenue - IRES-relevant, potentially VAT-able at 22%, with invoicing obligations.
2. **Art. 79(5) contamination.** Reclassified donations swell the commercial side of the commercial/non-commercial prevalence test; if commercial prevails, the Association becomes an *ente commerciale*.
3. **Indirect distribution (Art. 8(2) CTS).** A donor extracting privileged free access traceable to their cash gift is an indirect return on the gift.

**Why full exclusion, not down-weighting:** a reduced coefficient still converts euros into economic benefit mechanically - it shrinks the synallagma, it does not remove it. Applied: **GT-3A.4A** (monetary/material support recorded for recognition and memory only; excluded from all access and claim scoring), mirrored in A5 §3.1 and A4 §4.5(d), and the "Financial or material gift" example weight removed from the Hub DNA Reckoning (A1-5). A5 §2.3's corrispettivo / non-corrispettivo distinction was already well drafted; the ledger channel was what undermined it.

## Item 2 - A4 claim reform (applied: rename + freeze + rotation + discernment)

- Resource-cost places to members sit within APS decommercialisation (Art. 85 CTS: activities in direct implementation of institutional purposes, against *corrispettivi specifici*, toward members) for direct-tax purposes; the market-rate band to the public is commercial and should be tracked as such.
- **Cost must be demonstrably cost**: the documented composition (A4 §4.2 - hub/hour, materials, food, logistics) must be kept honest; any margin converts the slot to commercial revenue.
- **"Auction" renamed** to *contribution-priority claim*: in a verifica, an "auction" for programme places reads as commercial allocation of services.
- The reform's substance (discernment place, supersession freeze, rotation) is fiscally helpful: it breaks the strictly mechanical score→benefit linkage and supports the good-faith, mutualistic character of the access rule - which matters for the Art. 17 question below.

## Item 3 - A6 relational contribution share (applied with the four conditions)

Contractual freedom among autonomous commercial parties; exposure is in the *how*. The four conditions, now in A6 §9.3 and the A6-3 Project Sheet block:

1. **Slice fixed ex ante** in the Project Sheet, before work begins - a retroactively-sized "qualitative" pot allocated among related parties is an income-shifting pattern (e.g. steering income toward a partner in *regime forfettario*).
2. **Objective, written validation criteria** in the Project Sheet (coordination, facilitation, relational stewardship are real, invoiceable services).
3. **Full fiscal documentation** - the slice flows through the same invoicing pipe as every other distribution (A6 §10.2; GT-12).
4. **Role separation** - a recipient who is also a volunteer member elsewhere takes the paid care work under a contract (A3 §8.2 pattern).

## Item 4 - Flex NRC (dropped)

Two layers, the deeper one a pre-existing F1 question:

1. **Characterisation of the NRC itself.** For internal holons it is an internal allocation - no tax event. But where the payer is an external legal entity (A6 Vehicle Entities, post-Emergence entities), a **contractually mandatory percentage paid in exchange for ecosystem benefits** is almost certainly a corrispettivo for services - not a liberalità (compulsory payments are never liberal). That makes it commercial income: *attività diverse* under Art. 6 CTS, which must stay **secondary and instrumental** within the DM 107/2021 limits (≤30% of overall revenues or ≤66% of costs), and VAT-relevant with invoicing. **This characterisation is the first question for the F1 advisor - everything else prices off it.**
2. **Uniformity is a defence feature.** A standard, published structure cost is easy to defend; differentiated rates per counterparty invite scrutiny (favourable rates to insider projects = Art. 8 indirect-distribution pattern; variable price for the same service complicates the VAT base). The altitude lens read the flat rate as blue rigidity; fiscally it is protective armour. **Dropped.** If ever revisited: only via published, objective, Board-resolved criteria in an LGR, never bilateral negotiation.

## Cross-cutting: the Art. 17(3) CTS question (for the F1 advisor, explicitly)

**A volunteer's activity may not be remunerated in any way, even by the beneficiary** - expense reimbursement only, and volunteer status is incompatible with any compensation. Much of what the Contribution Ledger records *is volunteer labour*, and the sliding-scale access rule converts recorded contribution into economic benefits (discounts up to free). The more mechanical the conversion, the more it resembles remuneration in kind for volunteering.

Current protections: no tiers, no perk schedule, no fixed hour→euro conversion, steward discretion in good faith (GT-3A.3), contribution "deliberately not reduced to hours alone" (GT-3A.4). The 2026-06-10 claim reform (discernment place, rotation) rebuilds discretionary character precisely where the numeric score had re-mechanised it. The advisor should opine on whether the residual linkage is defensible as mutualistic access criteria rather than compensation, and on any further softening needed.

## Two smaller flags for the same engagement

- **VAT reform for associations.** The repeal of the old *fuori campo IVA* treatment of member corrispettivi (D.L. 146/2021, deferred repeatedly - last verified deferral to 1 January 2026) may now be in force: member corrispettivi shift to VAT-*exempt* with registration/compliance obligations. Verify current status; it changes the compliance load of every pricing band even where no tax is due.
- **Art. 79(5) monitoring.** NRC streams + market-rate bands + partnership flows should be tracked annually against the prevalence test as a standing dashboard item, not discovered at year-end.

## Recommended F1 agenda (in order)

1. **Characterise the NRC** per payer type (corrispettivo vs contribution; Art. 6 / DM 107/2021 headroom; VAT treatment).
2. **Confirm GT-3A.4A** (the firewall) and the liberality documentation it protects.
3. **Opine on Art. 17(3)** vs the access rule, with the claim-reform mitigations in view.
4. **Confirm the A6 §9.3 conditions** suffice against income-shifting.
5. **Status-check the VAT reform** and set up Art. 79(5) monitoring.
6. Review the homeostat clauses already marked *(Pending F1)* (GT-4.3/4.3A/4.4A/4.6/4.8 and dependents) - the original F1 scope.
