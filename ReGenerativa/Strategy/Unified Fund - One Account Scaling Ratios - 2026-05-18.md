# Unified Fund - One Account, Scaling Ratios

*Proposal only. Nothing in the vault changes until decided. Collapses every fund and
reserve in the constellation into a single account with ratio-based allocations.
Supersedes and absorbs the funding mechanism of
[[Access Floor - Guaranteed Baseline and Sliding Upside - 2026-05-18]] (its Tier R
becomes one allocation ratio here; Tier U is unaffected — it never needed money).*

---

## The model in one line

> **One account — the ReGenerativa Fund — receives all network money, holds a
> self-scaling solvency buffer as a ratio of itself, and pays the rest out as grants to
> hubs, stewards, and elders by fixed ratios. No fixed-euro reserves anywhere.**

This replaces the Network Regeneration Fund + the ring-fenced Elder Fund + the Care
reserve + the proposed Floor Reserve with **one fund and one allocation table**.

---

## Money in (one account)

| Source | Contribution | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Partnership (A6) | NRC per GT-4 (≥5%, negotiable 3–10%) | unchanged |
| Intrapreneurship (A7) | **fixed 7% lead fee** on Net External Revenue | replaces the per-phase NRC; see flag F4 |
| Membership (A2) | €25/yr | **net of Art. 18 CTS insurance only** — see flag F2 |
| Sponsorship (A5) | 100% of the gift | clean inflow |
| Other economic activity / donations | NRC / full | "etc." |

All of it lands in the one account. No sub-accounts.

---

## Reserves as scaling ratios (the actual simplification)

No euro thresholds anyone has to define, maintain, or rescue. Everything is a **ratio of
what the fund actually holds**, so the fund can never promise more than it has — the
Elder-Fund / Care-reserve / Floor-Reserve over-promise problem is structurally
impossible here.

- **Solvency Buffer = R1% of the fund**, never allocated. This is the *only* reserve,
  and because it is a ratio it self-scales — there is no LGR-2 per-Elder figure, no
  Care minimum €, no Floor Reserve € to set or hold.
- **Distributable = fund − buffer.** Allocated by fixed ratios:

| Allocation | Ratio | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| Hub grants — ecological restoration, shared infrastructure, inter-hub mobility | H% | old NRF purposes |
| Steward grants — operating support + steward hardship | S% | Care (steward) + NRF |
| Elder grants — hospitality | E% | the Elder Fund |
| Member access floor (Tier R free places) | M% | the proposed Floor Reserve |
| Contingency / general hardship (Care, non-steward) | C% | Care reserve |

`R1 + (H+S+E+M+C of the remainder) = 100%`. When the fund is small every category
shrinks proportionally and automatically — one mechanism, no per-fund "below reserve,
suspend" rules. Grants in any category are capped at that category's share of the
current distributable; unspent share rolls forward.

---

## Money out — grants, on a fixed cycle

One grant cycle (e.g. seasonal or annual, Board-run), one application path, paid from
the one account against the ratios:

- **Hubs** — ecological/infrastructure/mobility grants (was: NRF purposes, scattered).
- **Stewards** — operating-cost support and hardship (was: Care Protocol + NRF).
- **Elders** — hospitality (was: Elder Fund; the A8 §4.2 auto-convene trigger becomes
  "Elder grant ratio exhausted" instead of a euro threshold).
- **Members** — the access floor's Tier R, funded as the M% ratio.

Reported as one set of figures in the *bilancio sociale* — simpler audit than today's
four separate reserves.

## Honest flags (these are not optional reading)

**F1 — Italian Third Sector law is the hard constraint.** ReGenerativa is an APS-ETS
under D.Lgs 117/2017. An ETS **cannot distribute profit to members**, and "grants back
to stewards/elders/members" must be genuinely mission-bound mutual aid / cost
reimbursement / welfare — documented, and reported in the *bilancio sociale*. Steward
grants especially risk recharacterisation as disguised remuneration, colliding with
Agreement 3 §8 (non-employment, withholding, occasional-collaboration thresholds). This
needs the Association's legal/fiscal advisor before any GT edit. It does not block the
*architecture*; it constrains how "grant" is defined and paid.

**F2 — Membership fees are largely pre-committed.** The €25 covers Art. 18 CTS
insurance; only the net surplus is genuinely redistributable. Model it as inflow, but do
not count the gross as grant-able.

**F3 — One fund removes hard ring-fencing.** The Elder Fund was ring-fenced so it could
not be raided; ratios are *soft* protection. Acceptable and far simpler, but it is a
real trade — name it knowingly.

**F4 — The 7% intrapreneurship lead fee reverses an earlier decision.** The 2026-05-17
simplification set Intrapreneurship Phase 2/3 NRC to a flat 5%. This proposal sets a
**fixed 7% lead fee** (higher than the 5% standard — justified as the price of the
network having incubated the venture). Deliberate reversal; confirm. Open sub-question:
does the post-graduation NRC tail (A7-3, ≥5% for 3–5y) fold into the 7% lead fee or
remain a separate tail?

**F5 — Concentration.** One account concentrates control and audit in the Board. Mitigated
by fixed published ratios + the bilancio sociale, but governance oversight of the single
account matters more than it did with separated funds.

*after* the F1 review clears the grant definitions. Until then nothing in the vault
