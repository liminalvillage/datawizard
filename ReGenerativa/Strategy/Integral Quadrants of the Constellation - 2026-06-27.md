# Integral Quadrants of the Constellation

*Proposal and lens only. Nothing in the vault changes here, and no agreement, GT clause, or companion doc is edited until shown and approved. This note reads the Regenerative Covenant (GT-2) and its assessment through the **quadrants** line of integral theory (Wilber's AQAL), as a companion to [[Developmental Altitude of the Constellation - 2026-06-06]], which used the **levels** line (Spiral Dynamics). The altitude pass asked "at what altitude is the system holding value?"; this pass asks "across which perspectives is the system attending, and which does it under-attend?" It is a map for deciding, not a set of edits.*

> **Relationship to the altitude pass.** Spiral Dynamics and AQAL quadrants are two different lines of the same framework: one reads *developmental depth* (purple to turquoise), the other reads *irreducible perspectives* (I / We / It / Its). They are complementary, not redundant. The altitude pass found the constellation is "green with a yellow/teal skeleton, reaching toward turquoise." This pass finds the constellation is "all-quadrant in content but Right-hand in instrumentation" - and, usefully, the two diagnoses converge on the **same remedy**.

---

## Why this lens

The covenant's six dimensions (GT-2.2) are a strong map. But they are sorted by **domain** - ecology, health, knowledge, economy, community, governance - the way a sustainability framework lists topics. Integral theory offers an orthogonal cut: sort instead by **perspective** - the irreducible first-, second-, and third-person ways any whole can be known.

- **Upper-Left (UL) - "I"** - interior individual: consciousness, meaning, felt experience, dignity.
- **Upper-Right (UR) - "It"** - exterior individual: bodies, behaviour, the measurable organism.
- **Lower-Left (LL) - "We"** - interior collective: culture, trust, shared meaning, the pre-contractual bond.
- **Lower-Right (LR) - "Its"** - exterior collective: systems, structures, flows, institutions, ecology-as-system.

The wager of AQAL is that all four are real, none reduces to another, and a healthy practice attends to all four. That is exactly the covenant's own claim in different words: the mandate is "integral... not satisfied by ecological improvement alone, nor by human flourishing alone" (GT-2.1).

---

## The six dimensions re-sorted by quadrant

| Quadrant | Perspective | Covenant dimensions that land here |
|---|---|---|
| **UL** - interior individual ("I") | meaning, dignity, inner well-being | **B** (the emotional / psychological / felt half) |
| **UR** - exterior individual ("It") | bodies, behaviour, measurable health | **B** (the physical / safety / food half); **A** (organisms, soil, species as living bodies) |
| **LL** - interior collective ("We") | culture, trust, shared meaning | **E** (community / relational); **C** (knowledge as living culture); **F** (the integrity / value-alignment half) |
| **LR** - exterior collective ("Its") | systems, structures, flows | **D** (economy / infrastructure); **A** (ecological *systems*); **F** (governance *structure*) |

The first thing the re-sort reveals: **the six are not an orthogonal basis.** B straddles UL+UR, F straddles LL+LR, A straddles UR+LR. They are a legible domain checklist - good for a steward's one-page seasonal sheet - but not "simple" in the structural sense. That is fine; it just means the six are the *operational surface*, and the quadrants are the *structure underneath* that tells you whether the surface is balanced.

---

## The simplest honest cut: three perspectives

Integral's "1-2-3 of being" collapses the four quadrants into three irreducible perspectives, and the covenant maps onto them almost in the constellation's own words:

- **I - the interior of each being.** Leaving people "more whole" (B-interior). *Dignity, meaning, rest.*
- **We - the space between us.** "The village is the reference model... the pre-contractual bond" (GT-2.2e; E, C, the integrity of F). *Trust, culture, relationship.*
- **It / Its - the bodies, the land, the systems.** "Leave the land more alive," the economy, the infrastructure, governance-as-structure (A, D, F-structure, B-physical). *The measurable world.*

So: **six domains collapse to three perspectives** with little loss, because the covenant already insists all three move together.

---

## What the lens catches (the payoff, not the tidiness)

The value of this lens is not fewer boxes. It is that the quadrant cut names the constellation's **single biggest known failure mode** with precision - the same one the altitude pass called its *master pattern*.

The altitude pass: *"sophisticated yellow/orange machinery - the homeostat, the contribution ledger, contribution-weighted envelopes, the sliding scale - but the relational, dignity, and sensing substrate that makes such machinery safe is largely assumed rather than scaffolded... the metric eats the gift."*

In quadrant terms that is exactly: **the network over-instruments the Right-hand (It / Its) and assumes the Left-hand (I / We).** The evidence is in the Regenerative Impact Framework's own assessment methods:

- **Right-hand, crisply measured:** Dimension A (soil OM%, species counts, meter readings), Dimension D (cost-coverage %, NRC flows, Council Allocation demand). Numbers, baselines, targets.
- **Left-hand, admittedly soft:** Dimension B-interior, E, and F-integrity, where the Framework concedes the dimension "resists quantification most strongly" and falls back to conversations, end-of-stay reflections, and the question *"who here would you call in an emergency?"*

The Framework *knows* it is lopsided. AQAL tells you *why* (two of four quadrants are interior and cannot be measured from the outside, only sensed and dialogued from within) and gives the governing rule:

> **Never let a Right-hand proxy stand in for a Left-hand reality.** A soil test (UR) is not ecological *relationship* (LL). A contribution score (LR) is not *belonging* (LL). A cost-coverage percentage (LR) is not *dignity* (UL).

This is the same error, named structurally, that the altitude backlog kept surfacing item by item: the mentorship "auction" pricing a gift (LR proxy over LL/UL substrate); dynamic partner shares starving invisible care work (LR measure can't see LL contribution); the sliding scale putting the highest price on the lowest-capacity member (LR curve over UL dignity floor).

**The convergence is the finding.** Two independent integral lines - levels and quadrants - point at one corrective: put a non-contingent floor and a sensing fallback **beneath and around the metric layer.** And the two genuinely turquoise upgrades the altitude pass already proposed are, in this language, precisely the moves that restore the under-attended quadrants:

- the **Voice for the Land and the Unborn** (A8-1 §1.6; now in GT-2.7(f)) - standing for the more-than-human and future generations, the part of It/Its and We that has no present speaker;
- the **attunement / sensing-circle fallback** - a LL (collective-interior) practice for when a Right-hand score is contested or sensed to be misaligned.

AQAL did not invent these; it explains why they are the right two, and predicts there are no others of equal leverage, because they are the only two quadrants currently read by proxy.

## Recommendation

**Do not replace the six dimensions with quadrants.** That would trade operational legibility for theoretical elegance - a steward can act on "soil, food, waste" far better than on "Upper-Right exterior-individual," and the six map onto annexes, agreements, and seasonal practice already built. Transcend-and-include instead:

> **Keep the six dimensions as the operational surface. Add the three-perspective / quadrant balance check as the meta-structure that audits the six for balance** - one short addition to the Regenerative Impact Framework's "How to use" section, asking each season: *are we attending to all three perspectives (inner / between / outer), or only the measurable one?*

This makes the Framework's own stated worry - "this dimension is where the gap between stated values and operational reality most often appears" (Dimension D note) - into a **structural check** rather than a hope. It is additive, breaks no scaffolding, changes no money flow, and is therefore **not F1-gated**: a candidate for early, low-risk adoption alongside the substrate moves from the altitude pass.

### The proposed Framework addition (for review before any live edit)

A short subsection to sit after "From assessment to response" and before "What the Framework Is Not":

> **### A balance check across perspectives**
>
> The six dimensions can be read not only by domain but by *perspective* - the inner life of each being (dignity, meaning, rest), the life between us (trust, culture, belonging), and the outer world of bodies, land, and systems (ecology, economy, infrastructure). The outer perspective is the easiest to measure and so the easiest to over-weight: a soil test is not ecological relationship, a contribution score is not belonging, a cost-coverage figure is not dignity. Each season, alongside the six-dimension review, the steward asks one balancing question: *are we attending to all three perspectives - inner, between, and outer - or only the measurable one?* Where the honest answer is "only the measurable," the lagging perspective is named as what needs attention, and a relational or sensing practice (a reflection round, a conversation, a sensing circle) is the response - not a new metric.
