# Developmental Altitude of the Constellation

*Proposal and lens only. Nothing in the vault changes here, and no agreement or GT clause is edited until the F1 legal/fiscal review gate is cleared (same posture as [[Membership as Stewardship and the Common Reservoir - 2026-05-30]]). This note reads the whole constellation through a developmental-altitude (Spiral Dynamics) lens, corrects a common misread, names one master pattern, and lays out a tagged backlog of improvement opportunities. It is a map for deciding, not a set of edits.*

> **Application status (2026-06-10).** The backlog has since been applied in tiers, each recorded in the [[0.3 Decision Log]]: **SAFE-NOW** (11 substrate moves) applied 2026-06-07; **GOV-REVIEW** (Elder Emeritus, allocation challenge loop, money quorum, decadal valve, empty chair) applied 2026-06-08, with an integrity-review pass fixing two defects the same day; the **F1-FISCAL** items were pre-reviewed from a commercialista perspective ([[F1 Pre-Review - Fiscal Notes - 2026-06-10]]) and applied 2026-06-10 — the money-to-access firewall as **full exclusion** (GT-3A.4A), the A4 reform as the renamed **contribution-priority claim** with freeze, rotation, and discernment place, and the A6 relational slice with ex-ante conditions. The **flex-NRC item (Theme E, A6 §10A.2) was dropped** on fiscal advice: rate uniformity is a protective feature, not blue rigidity — the lens misjudged that one. All edits remain unsigned drafts pending the formal F1 review.*

## Why this lens

The lens is Spiral Dynamics, used diagnostically. Each altitude is a way of holding value, and the principle that matters here is **transcend-and-include**: a higher altitude never discards the scaffolding of the one below, it adds capacity on top. The altitudes, in brief:

- **Purple** - kinship trust, safety in belonging, the pre-contractual bond.
- **Red** - power, dominance, whoever is strongest takes.
- **Blue/Amber** - order, explicit rules, hierarchy, legal form, duty.
- **Orange** - achievement, metrics, markets, strategic optimisation.
- **Green** - community, egalitarianism, care, consensus, pluralism.
- **Yellow/Teal** - systemic, holonic, subsidiarity, functional-fit, self-tuning.
- **Turquoise** - holistic field-sensing, more-than-human standing, multi-generational and planetary horizon, inner development as infrastructure.

## The misread: this is not "deep blue"

Read by content rather than form, the constellation is already **green with a yellow/teal skeleton**, reaching in places toward turquoise:

- **Green** runs through it: agape as operating principle, the common reservoir, "leave it more alive," one member one vote, "abundance not gatekeeping," doubt resolved toward wider access (GT-3A.3 interpretive principle).
- **Yellow/Teal** is the architecture: the holonic principle, subsidiarity (GT-4.5(a)), the integral six-dimension covenant (GT-2.2), and the self-tuning living balance whose intervention is graduated, reversible, and "triggered by the Asset's measured balance, not by judgement of person" (A3 §5A.3).
- **Turquoise gestures** already exist: the "community ark" (GT-2.3), holons nested up and down, Eldership as "shade, soil, mycelium, the conditions for next growth" (A8 §4.4).

What is genuinely **blue** is the *form, not the worldview*: numbered clauses, the Statute > agreement > GT hierarchy (GT-11.2), jurisdiction, the APS-ETS vessel. And that blue exoskeleton was chosen *deliberately and correctly* - the Introduction names exactly why: *"the relational economy needs clarity to survive its own growth."* A green/yellow body in a blue skin, on purpose.

So the real question is not "blue to turquoise." It is: **what does turquoise add on top of an already-integral system, without discarding the scaffolding that lets it hold weight at scale?**

## The master pattern

One diagnosis sits under most of what follows:

> The network has built sophisticated **yellow/orange machinery** - the homeostat, the contribution ledger, contribution-weighted envelopes, the sliding scale, dynamic partnership shares - but the **relational, dignity, and sensing substrate** that makes such machinery safe *at altitude* is largely **assumed rather than scaffolded.**

When the numbers get hard, there is almost nowhere the system is *designed* to fall back to relationship. Absent that fallback, altitude collapses *downward*: to **red** (whoever logs most, or sits on the terminal council, takes power) or **purple** (the most-present in-group quietly protects itself). The remedy shape is consistent across nearly every finding and is pure transcend-and-include: **add a non-contingent floor and a sensing fallback beneath and around the metric layer.** It breaks no blue scaffolding.

## Theme A - The metric eats the gift *(orange capture corroding green/purple)*

The most consistent cross-document pattern. Where a gift or a care relationship is narrated in green/purple grammar but allocated by an orange score, the score becomes the thing people optimise, and the gift becomes a prize won by the highest accumulator.

**[NEW] A4 §4.5 - The mentorship "auction."** Resource-cost (free-mastery) places are allocated by a contribution-weighted auction; *"any member in good standing may claim... A member with a strictly higher contribution score may supersede a current claimant at any time"* (§4.5c), with a 48-hour "soft landing" to a paid place or release (§4.5e). A gift narrated as a leaderboard, with last-minute bumping. **Move:** reserve at least one place per Programme for the Mentor's discernment or hub-circle nomination by *need/readiness*, explicitly outside the score; freeze supersession before the 7-day logistics window rather than "up to commencement"; add an anti-hoarding rotation so a recent claimant is de-prioritised next time and the gift circulates. Rename away from "auction" - market grammar over a gift.

**[NEW] A5 §3.1 to A4 §4.5 - The money-to-access channel.** A narrow firewall exists: underwriting a slot's direct cost *"does not affect Resource-cost eligibility"* (A5 §3.2; A4 §4.5f). But the general channel is open: *all* support is recorded as a *"Validated Contribution of the Supporter"* (A5 §3.1), which feeds a supporter-member's contribution score, which sets auction priority (A4 §4.5c/d). Cash can buy the gift through the front door even though it cannot through the side door. **Move:** weight monetary/sponsorship contribution differently from - or exclude it from - the score that governs A4 access priority. State plainly that the auction reads non-monetary, labour/relational contribution.

**[NEW] A6 §9 + §7.2 - Dynamic shares starve invisible work.** Partner shares are *"calculated dynamically based on cumulative Validated Contributions"* (§9.1), while measurement defaults to *"hours, fixed milestones, agreed value units, market replacement value"* (§7.2). §7.1 lists "facilitation, relationships" as contributions, but the measurement menu is all orange-measurable, so care, holding, and field-tending are structurally under-counted, and the "syntropic collaboration" of §1.3 drifts toward a billing competition. **Move:** reserve a defined slice of Net Project Revenue for relational/stewardship contributions validated *qualitatively* (peer recognition), and require §8 validation to actively surface under-logged care work, not just accept what was logged.

**[KNOWN - F1] The ledger as a power instrument.** The Membership-as-Stewardship note already flags this directly ("Making contribution drive allocation, not only access, raises the stakes on contribution measurement") and proposes decentralising value-tracking to each holon plus an appeal path. The items above are the *agreement-level surfacings* of that same risk; they should be handled with the same mitigation.

**[REFINES INTENT] A2 §2.6 sliding scale + the dignity floor.** *"The more a Member contributes... the lower their price... up to free."* A clean orange incentive curve over a gift substrate, with the lowest-capacity members facing the highest price. A network-level floor already exists (GT-3A.8: priority booking + no-turn-away with spare capacity), which is good. The gap: that floor is not carried into the A4 mentorship auction, and is not named as a dignity baseline that the "first yes" of belonging confers independent of contribution. **Move:** make GT-3A.8 the explicit floor *beneath* the A4 auction, and name a non-contingent membership baseline so the slide operates only *above* dignity.

## Theme B - Power without counter-power *(red shadow at the top)*

The Eldership review was blunt: the *content* reaches for turquoise, but the *form* installs an amber life-tenure in-group as terminal Covenant-judge **and** surplus-allocator, with no external check. This is the system's single largest unmitigated power concentration.

**[NEW] A8 §8.1 / §8.4, Charter §1.3 / §6.3 - Life tenure with no renewal.** *"This Agreement is permanent... not subject to termination"* (§8.1); *"The network does not revoke Eldership"* (§8.4); a seat ends only on *"voluntary withdrawal... or death"* and *"No replacement is appointed - the Council's composition is determined entirely by who holds Agreement 8"* (Charter §6.3). Gerontocracy by structure, and in tension with the agreement's own "become the conditions for next growth" language. **Move:** decouple the *honour* (keep it permanent and irrevocable) from the *active governing seat*. Introduce an **Elder Emeritus** status - full honour, hospitality, named legacy, no allocation vote - and a renewal/affirmation cycle for active Council participation. This makes the "become soil" metaphor structural rather than poetic, and cures ossification without touching the gift.

**[NEW] Charter §5.5 vs §5.1.4 - Judge with no one to judge it.** The Council both names network drift and directs the terminal surplus. The Association *must* publicly answer a Covenant Signal within 30 days (Charter §5.1.4), but **no symmetric mechanism lets the network challenge a Council allocation**: §5.5.4 records decisions in the Council Record and the annual *bilancio sociale*, and §5.5.5 anti-self-dealing guards only *personal* payments, not *directional* capture. **Move:** mirror §5.1.4 for the Allocation bucket - contemporaneous (not just annual) posting of allocation decisions, and a defined member/steward quorum may lodge a query the Council must answer publicly within 30 days.

**[NEW] Charter §4.1 + §3.2 + §5.5.4 - Money governed more loosely than speech.** Covenant decisions need a two-thirds majority of *all* active Elders (§4.2); routine allocations need only a *"simple majority of those present"* (§4.1) on a body in *"continuous session"* (§3.2) with 48 hours' notice. A habitually-present subset can steer the surplus. **Move:** for Allocation-bucket decisions, raise the floor to a minimum participation threshold of all active Elders (or lengthen notice for money matters). Speech is already better protected than money; invert that.

**[NEW] A8 §2.2 - Tenure as a proxy for wisdom.** Eligibility is *"at least one other agreement... for a minimum period... currently five (5) years"* (via LGR-1). Duration is a blue/duty metric standing in for a turquoise quality. **Move:** keep the tenure floor as a threshold of belonging, but add a qualitative dimension to nomination (§2.3): the nominating body must articulate the *specific stewardship-of-the-whole* the nominee has demonstrated, recorded in the Transition Rite record. Prevents Eldership becoming pure seniority entitlement.

**[KNOWN - F1] The Elder Council "network-tier standing" seam.** Already resolved in the strategy work (2026-06-01) as *recognition only*: feeding the reservoir raises access standing and the Eldership-eligibility clock but confers no allocation voice and no seat (A8 §4.2A; GT-4.8(d)). Noted here only so the altitude map is complete - the resolution is sound and consistent with the moves above.

## Theme C - Mechanism without relationship *(premature altitude / accountability laundering)*

**[REFINES INTENT] A3 §5A.3 vs §16 - The measured-removal seam.** The homeostat may *"re-tune the Asset first... and only as a last resort recompose or change its Stewards,"* an intervention *"triggered by the Asset's measured balance, not by judgement of person"* (§5A.3). The strategy note rightly prizes this: depersonalising intervention makes the most toxic act in any commons contestable against a number rather than a person. **The gap is not the intent - it is the missing relational completion.** §5A.3 has no cross-reference to the Clause 16 protections (30/90-day notice; right to respond), so a steward could be removed by the metric path in a way that routes around the dignity machinery the rest of A3 carefully builds - "the numbers made us do it." **Move:** state that the measured balance *initiates a review*, it does not *execute a removal*; cross-reference Clause 16's notice and right to respond; add a required relational conversation when the band is first breached, not only at last-resort recomposition. This *completes* the "measured, not personal" intent by giving the person a relationship to fall into.

**[NEW] A3 §1.4 / §1.6 - Unhedged optimisation language.** The principal mandate is to *"optimise resource inputs and maximise regenerative outputs"* and become *"capable of creating more value with fewer extractive inputs."* Standing alone, efficiency-orange framing can crowd out the goods that resist measurement - rest, fallow, conviviality, ecological slack - and a steward optimising to the metric may degrade the very regenerative substrate the network exists to protect. **Move:** add a sufficiency counterweight: "...within the carrying capacity and rhythms of the Asset and its people, honouring rest, fallow, and ecological limits as part of regenerative use - sufficiency over maximisation." Transcends-and-includes the optimisation language rather than removing it.

**[NEW]† A1 §3.9 - Six-month activation to material breach.** Reported by review: within six months the Association must appoint a Steward, schedule an activity, and communicate the Asset's existence, with persistent failure *"grounds for immediate termination."* Escalating a slow activation to material breach is disproportionate blue force that can punish exactly the under-resourced holons the network most wants to support. **Move:** keep the six-month check-in but default the failure path to *conversation and re-tuning* (mirroring the homeostat's "re-tune first, recompose last"), reserving termination for genuine abandonment. *(Verify against A1 §3.9 text before acting.)*

## Theme D - The whole has no voice *(missing transcend-and-include - the genuine altitude gain)*

This is where the real turquoise increment lives, and almost all of it is *addable* without disturbing the blue/yellow scaffolding.

**[NEW] No more-than-human or future-generation standing anywhere.** Land is consistently the *object* of the covenant - kept "sound," outputs "maximised" - never a party in itself. Most glaringly, the body that claims to "hold the whole" (the Elder Council) contains no voice for the whole: no land, no watershed, no unborn. **Move:** add an **empty-chair / "Voice for the Land and the Unborn"** function. The Charter already creates rotating functional roles (§1.4); add one whose duty is to speak the non-present stakeholders' interest into the record before any Covenant Signal (§5.4a) or Allocation decision (§5.5). Pair with a seven-generation horizon test in the Regenerative Impact assessment (GT-2.4). This is the single most defensibly turquoise upgrade.

**[NEW] No field-attunement fallback when metrics fail.** Every contested allocation escalates from metric to majority vote (A6 §8.3; Charter §4.1) - orange to green-proceduralism - never to collective sensing. The network's stated substrate (agape, the pre-contractual bond, GT-2.2e) is treated as background that "cannot be contracted," not as live infrastructure. **Move:** a short **attunement clause** (lives in GT, referenced across agreements): where a score, share, or auction outcome is contested or sensed to be misaligned with the gift/regenerative purpose, a facilitated sensing circle may pause and review it before it is finalised - metrics as servant, not master. Installs the network's own value as infrastructure rather than rhetoric.

**[NEW] Charter §6.5 - Sensing asked for, only voting provided.** The Council is asked to *sense drift* and *hold intent* (turquoise capacities) but the only named practice is an annual review of *"its own functioning"* (§6.5), with consensus explicitly optional (§4.3). **Move:** name a light collective-sensing / contemplative practice as governance infrastructure - a structured reflective round before any Covenant deliberation, and a periodic facilitated review of the Council's developmental health, not just procedure. Treat inner development as load-bearing, not optional.

**[NEW] A5 §3.2 / §4 - Sponsorship has no turquoise channel.** Every earmark destination is human/operational; recognition is *"name or logo at a supported hub,"* a public directory - orange visibility. **Move:** add to §3.2 a destination "regeneration of land, water, soil, or non-human living systems on their own behalf" and a multi-generational/endowment option (support held for future stewards, not spent now); add to §4 a non-promotional recognition mode (a living-history entry, a witnessed acknowledgment) for those who give in pure gift and decline visibility return.

## Theme E - Blue ossification *(rules that will rot)*

**[NEW] A3 §14.2 - A rigid cap over a seasonal intent.** The clause invokes seasonal rhythm ("equinox and solstice gatherings... natural coordination points") then clamps *"intervals not exceeding three (3) months"* on top - friction for a dormant tool, possibly too slow for an intensive hub. **Move:** functional-fit cadence "proportionate to the Asset's intensity of use, and no less often than annually, with seasonal gatherings as the default coordination points." Lets cadence self-tune, which is the actual yellow intent.

**[NEW] A4 §11A.2 - A perpetual non-compete inside a gift.** The Mentor shall not *"independently replicate a Programme... at a competing location within the same territory during the term of this Agreement"* - and a framework agreement can run open-ended, making this effectively perpetual. Conventional commercial-restraint grammar imported into a relationship the agreement itself frames as a freely transmitted gift (§11A.3 already softens the intent). **Move:** bound it - jointly-developed Programmes only, a sunset (~12 months post-Programme), conversation-first rather than flat prohibition. Reframe around protecting the gift cycle, not market share.

**[NEW] A6 §10A.2 - Flat NRC vs the network's own homeostat claim.** *"There is no per-Project negotiation of the rate... Absent a Board-ratified figure, the rate is 5%."* A fixed central percentage reads as orange overhead and sits oddly against a network that describes itself as a self-tuning homeostat. (The 3-10% band and parameters live in GT-4.6, so this is partly an existing-design surface.) **Move:** allow the rate to flex within the Board-set band by the Project holon's regenerative impact or capacity, with the flat rate as floor/fallback - adding yellow functional-fit on top of the blue backstop.

**[NEW]† A7 §4-5 - "Open holon" closed by incumbents.** Reported by review: the venture holon is open *"as a design principle"* (§4.2) yet incumbents control admission by simple majority (§5.2) and have a revenue-share incentive to keep it closed. **Move:** admission refusals in writing with reasons (mirroring the Association's own §3.2 duty), and a refused applicant may have the refusal noted against the openness principle. *(Verify against A7 §4-5 text.)*

**[NEW]† A7-3 §2 - Perpetual NRC with no relational valve.** Reported by review: post-Emergence NRC is *"locked perpetually as an undilutable share."* This is the strongest anti-extraction guard in the set and should be *kept* - but over an infinite horizon a frozen lien reads as extraction-in-reverse rather than living interdependence. **Move:** add a decadal good-faith relationship review that affirms continued regenerative conduct and keeps the *link* alive, while never reducing the rate. Keeps the floor, adds living relationship on top. *(Verify against A7-3 §2 text.)*

## The three highest-leverage moves

If only three are taken forward:

1. **Close the money-to-access channel (A5 to A4).** The clearest structural hole: cash should not buy the gift. Self-contained, low-controversy.
2. **Decouple Elder honour from active authority + add the allocation challenge loop (A8 §8 / Charter §5.5).** Cures the gerontocracy and terminal-power shadow - the system's largest unmitigated power concentration - without touching the gift.
3. **Add the empty chair + the attunement-fallback clause.** The two genuine turquoise increments, and together the substrate that makes all the orange/yellow machinery safe when the numbers get hard.

## Relationship to F1 and the Membership-as-Stewardship note

Most of Themes A's deeper layer and the homeostat mechanics in Theme C are already owned behind the **F1 legal/fiscal gate** and should move with it, not ahead of it. The genuinely *new* contribution of this lens is the substrate the strategy work does not cover: the **dignity floor** beneath the metric, the **attunement fallback** when metrics fail, **more-than-human and future-generation standing**, and the **Eldership-to-turquoise** increments (Themes B and D). These are largely additive and several are *not* F1-gated, because they add relational/ceremonial capacity rather than changing money flows - candidates for earlier, lower-risk adoption.

A useful separability mirrors the strategy note's own: **the relational/dignity/sensing layer is independently valuable and mostly F1-safe; the metric-weighting refinements are the contested part and stay behind the gate.**
