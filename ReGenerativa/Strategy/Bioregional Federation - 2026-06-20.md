# Bioregional Federation

*Design note, 2026-06-20. The vision behind the cross-entity limb of **GT-4.10** (Federation — holons compose into holons). The binding principle is in GT-4.10(b); this note holds the direction for the dedicated federation instrument that GT-4.10 defers to F1. Addresses Structural Gap **SG8** ([[Constellation Structural Gaps - 2026-06-16]]).*

---

## The insight

Federation is not a new mechanism — it is the constellation's own self-similar holon shape, applied one level up. A holon groups smaller holons; the network is itself an ordinary holon (GT-4.4C); so the network can in turn be a holon within a larger grouping. **Clustering and federation are the same operation; the only thing that changes across scales is the legal boundary it crosses.** Within one legal entity, co-located hubs form a Cluster (GT-4.10(a)). Across legal entities, the Association federates with peer associations, networks, and commons (GT-4.10(b)). Same shape, same non-hoarding overflow, same no-new-vote / no-property-right rails.

## Why federate

A single association cannot regenerate a whole watershed or bioregion alone. The mission — to leave a place measurably more alive (GT-2) — is bioregional in scale. Federation lets value-aligned entities in a shared place pool mutual aid, recognise each other's people, and respond together to needs none could meet alone, without any of them surrendering autonomy or legal form.

## What a federation instrument would cover

- **Membership and scope.** Which entities, which bioregion/watershed, on what mission-alignment test (echoing A6 §6.1 "no net harm" and the Regenerative Covenant).
- **Mutual recognition.** Recognition of contribution and Passports across entities (GT-3A) — so a contributor's standing travels — on terms each entity opts into; never a forced merge of ledgers.
- **Shared Demand Signals.** A federated Network Demand Register (GT-1.12/1.13): needs surfaced in one entity that another can answer.
- **Federated mutual aid.** Voluntary inter-entity support, modelled on the common reservoir (GT-4.8) but across legal persons — gift/grant flows that respect each entity's non-distribution constraint.
- **Governance of the federation.** A light, subsidiary coordination body (the federation is a holon, not a new sovereign): one-entity-one-voice or similar, never overriding a member entity's own governance; the seasonal gathering scaled up.
- **The firewalls hold.** The access firewalls (GT-3A.4 / 3A.4A) and non-distribution (GT-4.5(g)) bind federated flows exactly as internal ones; money never buys access or returns to members across the federation either.

## F1 questions

1. **Legal form of the federation** — a *rete associativa* / network of associations, a consortium, an APS/ETS second-level body, or a contractual coordination? Implications for each member's ETS status and the Art. 79(5) prevalence test.
2. **Cross-entity recognition and data** — sharing Passport/contribution data across legal persons is cross-controller processing: GDPR joint-controller or transfer basis (GT-5); the off-ledger identity mapping stays per-entity.
3. **Federated mutual aid** — inter-entity gift/grant flows: liberality (Art. 83) vs corrispettivo; no indirect distribution (Art. 8) across the federation.
4. **Competition** — entities that are also commercial Partners (Agreement 6) coordinating in one bioregion: competition-law sensitivity (cf. the ledger-visibility flag).

## Relationship to the constellation

- **GT-4.10(b)** is the binding principle; **GT-4.10(a)** (the Cluster) is the network's own internal node and its on-ramp to a federation.
- **Operational Procedures Section P** holds the interim mechanics (cluster coordination; case-by-case bioregional collaboration pending the instrument).
- This note is a map for deciding, not an edit; the instrument itself is drafted post-F1.
