### 2026-06-15 — Purchase-side valuation and value-sharing (A1 §6.3A)
Agent: Claude (Opus 4.8)

Design session resolving the parked owner decision flagged in the last "What's next": the valuation method for the **A1 §6.3A(b) standing purchase option** (the network buying a comodato Asset into permanent commons). Worked the full design conversationally, then captured it as a new **§9** in [[Regeneration Annex - Value Return on Sale - 2026-06-11]] (the sale-side mirror, same comodato-sale-purchase axis). Design only; clause drafting stays *(Pending F1)*.

Decisions reached, in order:
- **Price** = `baseline x OMI`, premium-excluded — one valuation engine for both sale and purchase, so an Owner can't game the gap. On a standing-option purchase there's no sale price, so the formula is taken directly (the appraisal cancels).
- **Gratuitousness preserved** by framing the discount as *network-generated value the network declines to repurchase* (no Owner-to-Association transfer), plus making the option **contingent/event-armed** (dormant until triggered), with recital + Art. 1424 conversion clause as backstop.
- **Regenerated value `V`** has two estimators — `V_m` (formula) sets the number, `V_c` (tracked contribution) is evidence + cross-check + valuer tie-break.
- **`V` splits network/Owner**, both cash; the Owner share is the fairness dial. No steward euro (Art. 8 / 17(3) wall).
- **Steward benefit = allocation right via the historical stewardship signal** through the existing GT-4.3A value-system pipe — no new instrument.
- **Network share routes to the Asset Reserve** (capital, non-overflowing), not the operating band — caught that a capital-scale `V` dropped in the band would spill straight to the Elders past `nC`. With `R` reconciled to the formula price, the reserve absorbs `V` toward the path-to-commons ratchet (GT-4.4B(f)). Mission-locked over steward-directed, chosen for needing no new clause.

**Files updated:** `ReGenerativa/Strategy/Regeneration Annex - Value Return on Sale - 2026-06-11.md` (added §9.1-9.7 + F1 questions 7-9; frontmatter updated, operator, edit_log).
**Status:** complete — design captured; all *(Pending F1)*. Branch `fablesimplification`, not pushed.

### Learnings

- **pattern-discovered**: Regenerated value is a *capital* event and must not flow through the *operating* allocation band — the band is sized to `nC` (a multiple of operating cost), so a property-scale `V` overflows almost entirely to the Elder Council. The fix is the non-overflowing Asset Reserve (GT-4.4B), which also requires `R` to track the formula acquisition price, not replacement cost, or the reserve can't fund the buy. (See Regeneration Annex §9.5-9.6.)
- **pattern-confirmed**: Owner steers to principle-level, reuse-existing-pipes design — rejected a bespoke steward-allocation line with cap/sunset in favour of feeding the historical stewardship signal through the existing value-system-to-allocation pipe and routing capital to the existing reserve. Consistent with the `regenerativa-design-preferences` memory.
- **edge-case**: Feeding a *historical/lifetime* signal into an *allocation* right (normally active/recent) is a deliberate scoped stretch — kept on the allocation side of the GT-3A.4 money-to-access firewall; logged as F1 Q8.
