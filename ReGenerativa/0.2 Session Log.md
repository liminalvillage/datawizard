### 2026-04-14 — Operational Procedures companion doc created
Agent: Claude

**Created: [[Operational Procedures]]** v1.0

**Purpose:** Single companion document where operational time periods, workflows, and procedural details live — separated from the substantive rights and obligations in signed agreements. Amendable by Board resolution without re-execution of any agreement.

**Architectural principle established:** "Agreements hold what is substantive; procedures hold what is operational. Both are binding within their domains."

**12 sections, organised by domain:**
- A: Membership (annual cycle, resignation)
- B: Stewardship (notice, periodic review, hub activation)
- C: Mentorship (publication, registration, framework continuity, cancellation)
- D: Intrapreneurship (demand signals, activation, introductions, transitions, remedies, termination, NRC remittance)
- E: Partnership (distribution, withdrawal, confidentiality, validation workflow)
- F: Ownership (notice, transfer, maintenance reporting)
- G: Eldership (Council convening, authorities procedure, Charter amendment)
- H: Continuity & Dissolution (successor identification, Custodial Council, archive)
- I: Network Passport (thresholds, exit summary, tier review)
- J: NRF (rates, Elder Fund sub-reserve)
- K: Validation Platform (currently in A6-6, migration planned)
- L: Care Protocol cross-reference

**Each entry marked either:**
- "(Migration Pending)" — currently in agreement; this document records for reference until migration
- "(binding in agreement)" — should remain in agreement (substantive right protection); not migration candidate
- Unmarked — operational, naturally lives here

**Migration roadmap section** prioritises:
1. Mentorship (A4) — highest near-term tuning frequency
2. Intrapreneurship (A7) — many tunable timeframes
3. Membership (A3) — small contained set
4. Validation Platform (A6-6) — entire annex migrates here, A6-6 retired

**Migration is NOT executed in this turn** — only the procedural document is created. Each migration requires:
1. Agreement amendment to insert "per the Operational Procedures" clause
2. Confirmation that the timeframe in this document remains correct
3. Re-execution of the agreement (or, if Elder Council constituted, Integrity Review per A8 §5.4(b))

- 🌿 **Sprout** — establishing, growing into role
