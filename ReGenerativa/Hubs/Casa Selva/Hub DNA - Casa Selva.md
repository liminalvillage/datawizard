## The Place - Who We Are

Casa Selva is a sanctuary for conscious ways of being human — a regenerative sanctuary for conscious evolution, supporting the rise of a new humanity. Our vision is simple and large: *midwifing the passage to wholeness*.

Rooted in the mountains near Ascoli Piceno at around 500 metres, Casa Selva is held by the presence of **forest, mountain, and water**. These elements are not a backdrop but active co-healers — each contributing a natural frequency that supports grounding, restoration, and vitality. The place invites stillness: a chance to regulate the nervous system, reconnect with the body, and remember a slower rhythm. Nature here is an active partner — people are invited not only to *be* in nature, but to care for it and co-create with it.

Casa Selva exists as a physical place where inner transformation is practised through shared living, reflection, and collaboration — not just in theory but as part of a living system. It places emotional intelligence, self-awareness, and relational depth as the foundation for outer impact: a place where mutual accountability turns inner work into visible change, expressed as an embodied, regenerative upward spiral.

We rest on four pillars:
- **Inner work as soil** — if we don't work on ourselves, we cannot truly regenerate society or the environment.
- **Frameworks lived, not studied** — Internal Family Systems, anthroposophy, and Spiral Dynamics are woven into everyday life rather than taught as abstractions.
- **Feminine in balance** — care, intuition, and relational depth take their place alongside action and structure.
- **Health as rhythm** — illness is not the opposite of health but part of a natural process; healing emerges through the interplay of imbalance and restoration.

Casa Selva is designed to be rooted in collective care and resilient beyond any one individual — intended as a **living inheritance for future generations**, and part of a wider mycelial network closely connected to Liminal Village.

## The Covenant - What We Ask

### Presence and Participation
At Casa Selva we each take responsibility for the whole, contributing through time, presence, skills, or financial support. Leadership begins at home — in how we show up, how we speak, and how we take responsibility. This is a place for growing into mature adulthood, supported by deep intergenerational learning. Engage fully: share daily tasks, support the infrastructure and land, and be present in the life of the community.

### Care of the Place
- Leave every space at least as clean and ordered as you found it
- Report damage or concerns to the steward immediately
- Treat shared tools and infrastructure as your own
- Stewardship of the land is both a practical responsibility and a reflection of one's inner state

### Relational Standards
- Bring awareness to your emotional response; work through conflict; follow through on commitments
- Speak directly and honestly; do not gossip or triangulate
- Respect boundaries — physical, emotional, relational
- No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind
- No recording or photography of other participants without explicit consent

### Substances
The property is substance-free during retreats and residencies: no alcohol, cannabis, or other substances during facilitated or shadow-work sessions, and plant medicine is not offered on the property. This supports the place's focus on nervous-system regulation and healing, and is applied consistently with the [[Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct]] (GT-2.6) and Italian law.

### Children and Animals
Children are welcome with a responsible adult, within designated zones and times — fitting the hub's intergenerational-learning character. Animals are permitted by prior notice and steward approval, and remain the owner's responsibility at all times.

## The Rhythms - How We Live Here

### Cyclical timing
At Casa Selva, daily life follows the body, the land, the seasons, and the cycles of the sun and moon. Energy is understood as both limited and renewable; the day unfolds through a balance of rest and action, supporting regeneration rather than depletion. Every action — gardening, cooking, caring for others — becomes part of the path: *doing the work while doing the work.*
