## The Place - Who We Are

ReFactory is a co-creation space for **symbiotic innovation** — a thriving ecosystem where collaboration meets symbiosis. It brings together diverse minds and initiatives to forge new partnerships, innovative solutions, and regenerative practices; a place where third-sector entities and changemakers unite to create synergistic relationships that benefit both community and environment.

More than a workplace, it is a **living laboratory** for symbiotic innovation, new forms of collaborative economy, participatory governance, and regenerative partnerships. Nestled in the heart of the Marche region — the perfect blend of natural beauty and strategic accessibility — it sits ten minutes from Ascoli Piceno, with the Sibillini National Park, the Laga Mountains, and the Adriatic coast all within easy reach.

Three hectares, six thousand cubic metres, one hub: a place where time slows down and work resumes its natural rhythm, and where ecological practice is held not as a policy but as a lived standard.

## The Covenant - What We Ask

### Presence and Participation
ReFactory is more than a place — it is a movement where diverse minds unite to forge symbiotic relationships and sustainable futures. The community is the active protagonist: we build the calendar together. Bring your project, your skills, and your presence; contribution is participation in a shared ecosystem, not consumption of a venue.

### Care of the Place
- Leave every space at least as clean and ordered as you found it
- Report damage or concerns to the steward immediately
- Treat shared tools, workshops, and infrastructure as your own
- Compost, sort waste, and conserve resources — ecological practice as a lived standard, not a policy

### Relational Standards
- Speak directly and honestly; do not gossip or triangulate
- Respect boundaries — physical, emotional, relational
- No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind
- No recording or photography of other participants without explicit consent
- Workshop and maker-space safety rules apply: [to confirm]
