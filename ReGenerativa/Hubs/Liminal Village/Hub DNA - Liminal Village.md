## The Place - Who We Are

Liminal Village is a co-living and co-creation eco-tech hub in the centre of Italy. We have been experimenting with forming and weaving temporary communities aimed at creating value for the world — to heal themselves and heal the collective — by emphasising the balance of **personal, local and global regeneration**.

The word *liminal* comes from the Latin *limen*: threshold, a point of entering or beginning. A liminal space is the time between the "what was" and the "next" — a place of transition, of occupying both sides. We are an embodied experimental ground where value-aligned people come together to co-create and experience the future they would like to see: to "upgrade the operating system of society" and move from scarcity-based market economies toward *access abundance*.

We sit on the smooth hills of the Tronto Valley, with panoramic views over hills and mountains, conveniently between the mountains and the sea. We investigate the ecotones where things meet — Nature and Technology, Science and Spirituality, Economy and Society, Self and Community, Competition and Collaboration, Ownership and Commons — believing that *nature is the most advanced technology*, and that the victory worth seeking is the victory of the commons.

Together with other eco-initiatives around the village and around the globe, we are kickstarting a regenerative network of interdependent communities. Liminal Village is the heart of that network.

## The Covenant - What We Ask

### Presence and Participation
Coming here is not consumption. Every person present is part of what makes the place work. Contribute your presence (shared meals, communal work, openings and closings), offer your skills, and let yourself be fed, helped, and held.

### Care of the Place
- Leave every space at least as clean and ordered as you found it
- Report damage or concerns to the steward immediately
- Treat shared tools and infrastructure as your own
- Compost, sort waste, conserve water and energy as daily practice

### Relational Standards
- Speak directly and honestly; do not gossip or triangulate
- Respect boundaries — physical, emotional, relational
- No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind
- No recording or photography of other participants without explicit consent

### Substances
The property is substance-free, with the single exception of alcohol in moderation at shared social meals. Tobacco only in designated outdoor areas; no cannabis, other substances, or facilitated plant-medicine use on the property. Applied consistently with the [[Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct]] (GT-2.6) and Italian law.

### Children and Animals
Children are welcome with a responsible adult, within designated zones and times. Animals are permitted by prior notice and steward approval, and remain the owner's responsibility at all times.

### Lunar rhythm
