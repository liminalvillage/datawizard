## Section B — Restrictions and Excluded Uses

The following uses are **not permitted** without the Owner's prior written consent:

- Subletting or assigning the right of use to third parties outside the Association's network
- Use as a primary or permanent private residence by any individual
- Any use that generates revenue for private benefit rather than the Association's mission
- Structural modifications, extensions, or installations requiring planning permission
- Commercial activities unrelated to the Association's regenerative mission
- Use as security or collateral for any financial obligation
