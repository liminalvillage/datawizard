*Annex 5 to the [[Agreement 1 — Ownership|Ownership Agreement]]. Complete one per hub. The Hub DNA is the living expression of a hub's identity — its values, rhythms, expectations, and offerings. It is also referenced in the [[Agreement 3 — Stewardship|Stewardship Agreement]] (Clause 4) and the [[Agreement 4 — Mentorship|Mentorship Agreement]] (Clause 2.2). File completed documents as: "Hub DNA — [Hub Name].md" in the Hubs/[Hub Name]/ folder.*

*The Hub DNA is not a legal contract. It is a relational document — the first thing a new steward reads, the reference point a mentor consults before arriving, the promise made to every person who crosses the threshold. It should be written in the voice of the place.*

*Read alongside [[Agreement 3 Annex 1 — Steward Onboarding]] and [[Agreement 2 Annex 1 — Member Onboarding]]: the Hub DNA is the place introducing itself; those documents are the person introducing themselves.*

## The Covenant — What We Ask

*What a person commits to by coming here. Not rules — a covenant. The difference is that rules are enforced from outside; a covenant is held from within.*

### Presence and Participation

Coming here is not consumption. Every person present is part of what makes the place work — the cooking, the cleaning, the care of the land, the holding of the social field. We ask:

- **Contribute your presence:** Show up for shared meals, communal work sessions, and the opening and closing of gatherings. Absence without communication is a small failure of care.
- **Offer your skills:** What do you do well? We don't need you to be extraordinary — we need you to be present and willing.
- **Receive what is offered:** Belonging is a practice. Let yourself be fed, helped, held.

### Care of the Place

- Leave every space at least as clean and ordered as you found it
- Report damage, defects, or concerns to the steward immediately — not at the end of your stay
- Treat tools, equipment, and shared infrastructure as if they were your own — because while you are here, they are
- Compost, sort waste, and conserve water and energy as a natural part of daily life

### Relational Standards

This is a place where people are building something together. We hold these as minimum expectations, not aspirational ideals:

- Speak directly and honestly — about needs, difficulties, and appreciations
- Do not gossip or triangulate — if there is a conflict, address it with the person involved
- Respect boundaries — physical, emotional, and relational — without needing them to be explained
- No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind
- No unsolicited recording, photography, or documentation of other participants without explicit consent
