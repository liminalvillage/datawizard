*Annex 7 to the [[Agreement 1 — Ownership|Ownership Agreement]]. The **vehicle / equipment / power-tool care protocol** — the asset-specific child of the general [[Agreement 1 Annex 6 — Asset Care Protocol]] for the case where the Asset is a vehicle, a piece of powered or mechanical equipment, or a power tool. Forms an integral part of that Agreement; also referenced in the [[Agreement 3 — Stewardship|Stewardship Agreement]] (Clause 10). Final framings await F1.* *(Pending F1.)*

> **Read alongside [[Agreement 1 Annex 6 — Asset Care Protocol]].** The general parent holds what is true of caring for any Asset — the responsibility split (ordinary vs extraordinary; Steward / Association / Owner / user; the shared burden of [[Agreement 2 — Membership]] §4.6), the Asset register and condition, the maintenance cadence and log, damage reporting, and lifecycle. This child adds what a vehicle or piece of equipment specifically needs that a place or a parcel of land does not: a servicing and roadworthiness/calibration regime, safe-use and PPE rules, operator authorisation, and booking-and-return discipline. Care is purpose-bound to the Asset's **Asset DNA** (its Permitted Use record, [[Agreement 1 Annex 2 — Permitted Use and Regenerative Impact]]) — the asset is kept fit for the purpose and within the limitations for which it was entrusted.

---

## Section A — Scope

This Protocol applies to any Asset registered (A1-6 §C) under a **Kind** of *Vehicle*, *Power tool*, *Appliance*, or *Equipment*. A simple hand tool needs only the register entry and routine post-use care of the parent (A1-6 §D); use this Protocol where the item is powered, mechanical, road-going, or otherwise carries a servicing, safety, or operator-competence requirement.

Each such Asset has, in addition to its register entry, an **item care sheet** capturing the fields below. The Steward keeps these alongside the Asset register.

---

## Section B — Servicing and Roadworthiness / Calibration

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Service interval | Per manufacturer guidance or local practice (e.g. every X km / hours / months) |
| Last service | Date, by whom, summary of work |
| Next service due | Date or trigger condition (km / hours / season) |
| Roadworthiness (vehicles) | Registration, insurance, and statutory inspection (e.g. *revisione*) — current expiry dates tracked |
| Calibration / safety check (powered tools) | Interval and last/next date; PPE checked alongside the tool |
| Consumables | Fuel/charge, oil, filters, blades, belts — type and where replacements are sourced |
| Manuals & documents | Location of the manual, logbook, insurance, and warranty |

**Ordinary servicing** (routine service, consumables, cleaning, minor part replacement attributable to normal use) is the **Association's** responsibility, typically via the **Steward**, met from the Asset holon's cost-coverage bucket (GT-4.4(b)). **Extraordinary repair** (major mechanical failure or part replacement not attributable to misuse) follows the parent's ordinary/extraordinary split (A1-6 §B) — the **Owner's** responsibility where the Owner entrusted the Asset; or, for an Association-owned Asset, met from cost-coverage with top-up per GT-4.4(c) where needed.

An Asset that misses a due service, fails a safety/roadworthiness check, or is found in *Needs maintenance* / *Out of service* condition shall not be used until restored.

## Section C — Safe Use and Operator Authorisation

- **Who may operate.** The item care sheet names who is authorised to operate the Asset and any competence or licence required (e.g. a valid driving licence for a vehicle; demonstrated competence and induction for a power tool). No one operates an Asset they are not authorised for.
- **PPE and safe-use rules.** Required personal protective equipment and the item's safe-use rules are recorded on the care sheet and observed by every user.
- **Conditions and limitations.** Use stays within the Asset DNA (A1-2) — purpose, permitted uses, and any limitations the Owner set (e.g. "the van is not to be used off-network"; "the chainsaw is for trained operators only").
- **Incident handling.** Any near-miss, malfunction, or unsafe condition is reported immediately (A1-6 §E Damage Reporting) and the Asset withdrawn from use until checked.

---

## Section D — Booking, Use Log, and Return

For a shared vehicle or item of equipment, use is coordinated so it is available, accounted for, and returned sound:

- **Booking.** Use is booked through the Asset's agreed booking method; priority and pricing for contribution-dependent access follow the sliding-scale rule (GT-3A.3) where the Asset is a contribution-dependent resource.
- **Use log.** Each use records: user, date/time out and back, start/end odometer or hour-meter (where fitted), fuel/charge state out and back, and condition on return.
- **Return standard.** The Asset is returned clean, refuelled/recharged to the agreed level, with accessories and documents, to its designated location. Any new fault is flagged on return.
- **Inter-holon movement.** Lending the Asset to another holon follows the parent's lifecycle rules (A1-6 §F) and both Assets' DNA conditions; logged.
