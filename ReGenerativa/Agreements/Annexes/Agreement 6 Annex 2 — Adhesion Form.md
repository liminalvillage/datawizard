### Declaration of Adherence

By signing this form, the undersigned:

**1. Declares to have read and understood** the Partnership Agreement and all its annexes in their current version as of the date of this adhesion.

**2. Accepts unconditionally** all terms of the Partnership Agreement, including the General Terms and Conditions incorporated therein.

**3. Declares** that:
- They have full legal capacity to enter into this Agreement
- They are not subject to any existing obligations that would prevent or restrict their participation as a Partner
- They will comply with applicable Italian tax and regulatory requirements arising from their participation in Projects under the Framework
- No action is knowingly being taken that would place any Project in legal conflict

**4. Commits to** participating in the Framework in accordance with its principles of transparency, contribution-based fairness, autonomy, and regenerative efficiency.
