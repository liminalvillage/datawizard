*Annex 1 to the [[Agreement 3 — Stewardship|Stewardship Agreement]]. Complete one per steward per Asset, before or at the start of stewardship. Read alongside the Asset's completed Asset DNA — for a Hub, the [[Agreement 1 Annex 5 — Hub DNA]]; for another asset, its Permitted Use record ([[Agreement 1 Annex 2 — Permitted Use and Regenerative Impact]]). That document is the asset introducing itself; this one is the steward introducing themselves. File completed documents as: "A3-1 — Steward Onboarding — [Name] — [Asset Name].md".*

> **This template is the Hub (place) onboarding form** — its prompts (the slowness, the land, quiet hours, the place becoming what it can be) speak to stewarding a place. It applies as written when the Asset is a Hub. **For other asset kinds, asset-specific onboarding annexes branch from this one**, each keeping the same spirit (a stewardship intention, not a job description) while replacing the place-specific prompts with ones fitting that asset: [[Agreement 3 Annex 2 — Vehicle and Equipment Steward Onboarding]] and [[Agreement 3 Annex 3 — Land Care Steward Onboarding]] *(both dormant, in Archive — activate when the network stewards such an asset)*. Use the relevant branch where one exists; otherwise adapt this form, reading "hub" as "the Asset".

*This is not a job description. It is a stewardship intention — written before the role begins, reviewed each season, and completed with handover notes when the stewardship ends. It is read by the Association, the Owner (where relevant), and the incoming steward.*

*This is not a form. It is a conversation that happens on paper before the real conversation begins.*
