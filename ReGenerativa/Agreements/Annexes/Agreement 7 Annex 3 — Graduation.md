## Recitals

The Association and the Venture Members entered into the Intrapreneurship Agreement on [original date]. The Venture Holon has progressed through [Phase 1 / Phases 1 and 2 / all three phases] and has demonstrated the capacity to operate independently. The Parties now agree to formalise the Graduation of the venture on the terms set out in this Agreement.

---

## 1. Effect of Graduation

**1.1** From the date of graduation, the Intrapreneurship Agreement (including the Network-First Ruleset) ceases to apply to the graduating entity. The graduating entity operates as a fully independent legal entity.

**1.2** The following obligations **survive graduation** and remain binding on the graduating entity for the periods specified:

- Network Regeneration Contribution (Clause 2)
- Intellectual property licence to the Association (Clause 4)
- Preferred-client obligations (Clause 3, if applicable)
- Reporting obligations (Clause 5)

**1.3** The graduating entity retains eligibility to engage with the network as a Partner (Agreement 6) or Supporter (Agreement 5) on standard terms, and may access hub resources accordingly.

---

## 2. Post-Graduation Network Regeneration Contribution

**2.1** The graduating entity shall continue to pay a Network Regeneration Contribution on Net External Revenue for a defined post-graduation period, in recognition of the ecosystem support received during incubation.

**Post-graduation NRC rate:** ___% (minimum 5% per GT-4; may be higher by agreement)

**Post-graduation NRC period:** ___ years from graduation date (recommended: 3–5 years)

**Calculation base:** Net External Revenue — revenue remaining after deduction of direct costs, fiscal charges, and agreed operating costs, as defined in GT-1.9

**Payment frequency:** ☐ Quarterly  ☐ Annually

**After the post-graduation period:** The NRC obligation ceases unless the graduating entity chooses to continue as a Supporter (Agreement 5) or Partner (Agreement 6) with ongoing NRC obligations.

**2.2** Failure to pay the NRC during the post-graduation period constitutes a breach of this Agreement and may result in loss of preferred network access and, in serious cases, recovery of a portion of incubation value as specified in Clause 6.

## 7. Reporting

**7.1** The graduating entity shall provide the Association with an annual report for the duration of the post-graduation NRC period, covering:
- Total Net External Revenue for the year
- NRC amount calculated and paid
- A brief description of the entity's activities and its continued connection to the network's values

**7.2** The Association may request additional information if needed to verify NRC calculations. The graduating entity shall respond within 30 days.

---

## 8. Relationship Going Forward

**8.1** Graduation is not separation. The graduating entity is invited to remain part of the network ecosystem as a Partner, Supporter, or informal collaborator, on terms appropriate to its new status.

**8.2** The Association recognises the graduating entity's story as part of the network's story — its emergence, growth, and independence are a demonstration that the incubation model works. Both Parties commit to honoring that shared history in how they represent themselves to the world.

**8.3** The dispute resolution provisions of the General Terms (GT-7) apply to any dispute arising under this Agreement.
