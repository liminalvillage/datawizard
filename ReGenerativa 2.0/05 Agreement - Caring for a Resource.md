# Agreement - Caring for a Resource

*Between a **Steward** (a member) and **ReGenerativa APS**, for the place or asset named on its [[07 Sheets|Resource Sheet]] (the resource). Reads with the [[02 Rulebook]]. You are a trusted guardian of the resource, not a tenant, not a hirer, and not an employee.*

---

## 1. Your mandate

You care for the resource day to day: keep it sound and safe, keep it used for its purpose and within the owner's conditions, and hold its keys and records. Where it is a place, you also welcome the people who come and keep its culture (Rulebook 2.3). Where it is an asset, you keep it maintained, stored and insured, and available to the network on the terms you publish. Where the resource has several stewards, you hold one mandate together and decide by the method on the Resource Sheet.

## 2. Your authority

You direct the resource's money within the shape (Rulebook 4.2): after the slice and the costs, you decide the free spending up to the ceiling, alone under your own responsibility or together with your co-stewards, needing a second signature only above the amount in section 11 and always for your own reimbursement. You may say no to a specific person's access to this resource, with reasons, reviewable under Rulebook 9. You confirm contributions to the resource in members' records in good faith.

## 3. What the Association gives you

- The resource's envelope on the platform, with its share of the resource slice of every euro earned with it flowing into it.
- Reimbursement of your real, documented expenses within 60 days (Rulebook 4.6).
- The pool behind you: if the resource falls genuinely short beyond the months of tolerance, it is caught (Rulebook 4.7).
- If you live at the place and your mandate ends for any reason other than danger or misappropriation, help finding a place within the network within your notice window.
- Recognition: stewardship is recorded in your record and counts toward access and, in time, elder recognition.

## 4. What you give

- Your care, unpaid. If you also do paid work here (teaching, building, running a programme), that is a separate written services agreement or an [[06 Agreement - Running an Activity]], never this one (Rulebook 4.5).
- A short annual note to the Board on how the resource is doing (Rulebook 6.6), a seasonal look at the resource against the two promises of the [[01 Charter]], and attendance at the stewards' quarterly circle (Rulebook 6.3).
- A reconciled envelope and full handover when you step down.

## 5. When the resource struggles

Shortfall is handled by the numbers, not by judgement of you: free spending narrows, the gap heals over the following months, and beyond the tolerance the pool steps in and the costs target is re-tuned first. Only if shortfall persists after that may your mandate be changed, and then only with the notice and hearing of Rulebook 8 (Rulebook 4.7).

## 6. Ending

Either of us may end with the notice in Rulebook section 11 (30 days; 90 after a year in role). You hand over keys, records, and the reconciled envelope, and finish or hand on anything scheduled within the notice. Either of us may end at once for serious cause (Rulebook 8.5); for this role, serious cause also includes refusing to hand over keys or records. If the owner takes the resource back, Rulebook 8.1 protects you.

## 7. Legal footing

Voluntary activity under Art. 17 of Legislative Decree 117/2017; delegated spending authority (delega di spesa) from the Board under Art. 12 of the [[Statute - ReGenerativa APS|Statute]] and Rulebook 4.9. You are liable only for loss caused by your wilful misconduct or gross negligence and are otherwise held harmless by the Association.
