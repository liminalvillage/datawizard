# Agreement - Joining

*Between a **Member** and **ReGenerativa APS**. This is the door: every other role starts here. Reads with the [[02 Rulebook]]. Your [[07 Sheets|Welcome Letter]] is attached.*

---

## 1. Joining

You apply in writing, the Board decides without discrimination, and you are a member from the day it says yes. If it says no, it tells you why within 60 days and you may ask the Assembly to decide. Membership lasts as long as you want it.

## 2. What you get

- **A vote.** After three months, one vote in the Assembly, equal to everyone else's, and the right to stand for office and inspect the books.
- **Belonging.** Access to every resource and programme of the network under the access rule and the floor (Rulebook 5.4, 5.5): the more you contribute, the higher your priority and the lower your price, up to free; and you are never turned away from spare capacity only for inability to pay.
- **A record.** Everything you give is remembered in your record, portable across every resource and role (Rulebook 5.1).
- **A standing invitation to tend.** From your first day you may look after a project, a corner of a place, or an asset, as much or as little as you choose; where you do, you help direct that resource's free spending (Rulebook 3.3).
- **Support.** Mentoring, introductions, tools, and the attention of the group on what you are building; where the network carries you while you build, on a written support arrangement (Rulebook 7).
- **Insurance** for the volunteer activities you take part in, and the protection of the [[Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct]].

## 3. What you give

- The annual fee (Rulebook section 11), which covers your insurance and the Association's democratic life.
- Your contribution to the costs of any resource or programme you use, in money or in work, on the steward's published terms (Rulebook 5.3).
- Respect for the Statute, this Rulebook, the [[01 Charter]], and the conditions of each resource you use.
- Honesty in your record: you claim only what you did, and confirm others' contributions in good faith.

## 4. What membership never gives

No property right, no share of any surplus, no income, and no vote beyond the one every member has (Rulebook 3.5). Your belonging never depends on what you produce, and no one may condition it on personal favour.

## 5. Ending

Leave any time by writing to the Board; the fee for the current year stays due. The Board may exclude a member for arrears, breach of the Statute, or conduct against the purpose, with reasons, after hearing you; you may appeal to the Assembly within 30 days (Rulebook 8.3, 8.5). Your record stays yours; you receive a summary within 30 days.

## 6. Legal footing

Membership of a social promotion association under Arts. 21, 23 and 35 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 and Art. 5 of the [[Statute - ReGenerativa APS|Statute]]. Volunteer activity is unpaid; expenses are reimbursed against receipts only (Art. 17). Your data is held under GDPR (Rulebook closing note).
