# Charter

*What ReGenerativa is for, and the promises no rule may bend. One page. The rules are in the [[02 Rulebook]].*

---

## Why

Most of us have inherited a world where land is owned by few, work is separated from home, and care for a place is nobody's job. Places are used up. People are used up with them.

ReGenerativa is a way to do it differently at a scale that works: a single human life, a single place, and a network of both. Owners lend places and tools instead of selling them. People join, care for them, and earn from them. What they earn is shared by a rule everyone can read. What they give is remembered. No one can buy control, and nothing shared can be taken back out.

The Association that holds this is ReGenerativa APS, a non-profit social promotion association under Italian law. Its statutory purpose is the wellbeing of people and places through residencies, retreats, courses and events across a network of interconnected places, with circular-economy practice, collaborative governance, and a shared record of resources and contributions. The [[Statute - ReGenerativa APS|Statute]] is the legal text; this Charter is its spirit.

## Two promises

**To every place: leave it more alive than you found it.** Soil, water and living systems; the health of the people in it; the knowledge passed on; fair economics and shared tools; the community and its culture; honest and open governance. Every place reviews itself against these six each season, using the [[Regenerative Impact Framework]]. This is the Regenerative Covenant.

**To every person: leave them more free than you found them.** The network helps people build real livelihoods and real autonomy and self-actualisation. Support is never a debt. Failure is never a debt. No one's belonging depends on what they produce. When support helps create value, an agreed and bounded part of it returns so someone else can be supported next. This is the Mutual Prosperity Covenant.

## What nothing can ever buy

Whatever you give the network, in money, land, time or care:

- **it never buys a vote.** In the Association it is always one member, one vote.
- **it never buys ownership.** No role and no contribution creates a property right in anything the Association holds.
- **it never buys income.** The Association distributes no profit and no surplus to anyone, in any form, ever.
- **it never buys control over another person.** Support does not purchase authority.

What giving does earn is remembered in your record, and it lowers your price and raises your priority for what the network offers, up to and including free.

## How we are together

We decide as close as possible to where a decision lands. We keep money flows visible to every member. We prefer a conversation to a procedure, and a procedure to a judgement of persons. We measure success by increased freedom, not increased need. We treat every rule in the Rulebook as serving these promises, and when a rule produces an outcome that betrays them, we pause and look again before we finalise it.

## The invitation

Nothing here commits you to anything. Joining is one signature and a small annual fee. Every other role is one more short agreement, taken up when you are ready and set down when life changes. Read the Rulebook. Then, if you want to, come home.
