# Agreement - Running an Activity

*Between the **Activity Stewards** (members) and **ReGenerativa APS**, for the activity described on its [[07 Sheets|Activity Sheet]]. Reads with the [[02 Rulebook]]. One agreement for anything that brings money in: a course or retreat, a project for a client, or a venture. The Activity Sheet says which.*

---

## 1. The activity

You run the activity on the Sheet under the Association's roof: the Association is the legal person, holds the activity's envelope, issues every invoice and receipt, and collects every payment. You never collect money directly unless the Sheet says so in writing. The activity serves the network first and the wider world second; the Sheet states the share of your working capacity that goes to each.

## 2. Money

Every euro the activity earns follows the shape (Rulebook 4.2): the network slice, and the resource slice if lent resources (a place, an asset, or both) were used; then the activity's own costs (food, materials, logistics, payment charges); then what remains. The Sheet says how what remains is used:

- **For a course or retreat:** the teacher's agreed share (fixed fee, revenue share, or hybrid) is paid within 30 days of completion; the rest is the activity's free money under Rulebook 4.2, up to the ceiling, then the pool. Places on the course are priced in up to three bands: full price, member price, and a **cost-only band** for those who have contributed most, where the teacher's labour is the gift. Cost-only places carry no slice.
- **For a project or a venture:** the people who did the work share what remains in proportion to their recorded, validated contributions to the activity, recomputed as the work goes on, paid within 30 days of receipt and reconciliation. The Sheet may reserve, in advance, a fixed share for coordination and care of the collaboration. Each person documents their own income (Rulebook 4.5).

Nothing in this agreement is a distribution of the Association's surplus: what you receive is payment for services you delivered, documented under Italian fiscal rules, the Association acting as withholding agent where required.

## 3. What the Association gives

Fiscal hosting, invoicing, and the platform; promotion through the network; access to lent resources under their stewards and the access rule (Rulebook 5.4); for a venture in its first phase, the network as its first market and three introductions within 60 days; and mentoring and the group's attention on what you are building (Rulebook 7).

## 4. What you give

Honest recording of contributions and costs; respect for each resource's steward, conditions and culture; the pricing bands and the access rule applied in good faith; a note in the annual report of the resource or activity; and, where the activity uses lent resources, the resource slice, with no negotiation of the rate below the floor.

## 5. Growing and leaving

A venture may, once mature, become its own legal entity with the Association's written agreement, on a two-thirds vote of its members, settling what it owes and keeping paying the network slice as a service fee at a rate fixed on leaving (Rulebook 4.10). It keeps preferred access to the network for as long as it honours that. Nothing is distributed to departing members.

## 6. Ending

By decision of the activity's members, by twelve months' inactivity, by 30 days' notice from either side, or at once for serious cause (Rulebook 8.4, 8.5). Confirmed programmes and client commitments within the notice are completed or handed on. The envelope is reconciled; unvalidated contributions are settled within 60 days; the rest follows Rulebook 8.4.

## 7. Legal footing

Paid collaboration with a social promotion association within Art. 36 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 and the Association's fiscal-compliance rules (Rulebook 4.5, 4.9); no share in the Association's patrimony or surplus (Art. 8). Disputes between activity stewards go straight to external mediation (Rulebook 9.2, step 4) if steps 1 and 2 fail.
