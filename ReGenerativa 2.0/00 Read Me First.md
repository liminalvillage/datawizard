# ReGenerativa 2.0 - Read Me First

---

## What this is

ReGenerativa is a network of places (a farm, a house, a workshop, a piece of land) and assets (a vehicle, a tool, a set of equipment) lent to an association so that people can care for them, live and work with them, earn from them, and leave them more alive than they found them. Places and assets are together called resources; every resource has a steward.

The legal stack exists so that this can happen at many places, with many things and many people, fairly, without anyone being able to extract or enclose what was shared.

## Three ideas

Everything in the stack follows from these:

1. **A resource is lent, a person joins, someone cares for it.** Three roles: Owner, Member, Steward.
2. **Every activity that earns money runs one shape.** A slice goes to the network (and to the borrowed resources it used), costs are covered first, the people who care for the resource direct what is left, and lasting surplus rises to one shared pool that catches anyone who falls genuinely short.
3. **What you give is remembered and lowers your price. It never buys a vote.**

| [[05 Agreement - Caring for a Resource]] | The Steward's agreement | you will look after a place or an asset day to day |
| [[06 Agreement - Running an Activity]] | The agreement for anything that earns money: a course, a project, a venture | you will run something that brings money in |
| [[07 Sheets]] | The three fill-in sheets: Resource Sheet, Activity Sheet, Welcome Letter | you are signing one of the agreements above |
| The [[Statute - ReGenerativa APS\|Statute]] (unchanged, in 1.0) | The Association's constitution under Italian law | you need the legal foundation |

The agreements are short because they point to the Rulebook instead of repeating it. Where an agreement and the Rulebook differ, the agreement you signed governs for you; the Statute governs over everything.

## What changed from 1.0, and why

| 1.0 | 2.0 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 8 agreements | **4 agreements** | Mentorship, Partnership and Intrapreneurship were all "an activity that earns money using network resources". One agreement plus an Activity Sheet covers a course, a project, and a venture. Sponsorship is a gift, and a gift needs no counter-signed contract: it is a section of the Rulebook. Eldership is a recognition, not a contract. |
| Elder Council holds the common reservoir; Board stands in until the Council exists (~2029) | **The Association holds one shared pool.** Every allocation is posted; any three members can question one and get a public answer in 30 days | Removes a body, a charter, an interim clause and a quorum rule, while keeping the transparency. Elders keep their role as mediators through the Statute's Probiviri. |
| NRC and ARC, two contributions with override rules | **The slice**: one share to the network, one to the borrowed resources | Same principle, half the text, no negotiation per activity. |
| Asset (general) and Hub (place), two terms with an interpretation clause | **Resource = place or asset.** One word for what is lent, one lending agreement, one steward agreement, one sheet; rules that only fit a place say so | Both kinds get a steward and run the same shape, without a second set of documents. |
| Band of tolerance, backstop, ceiling multiplier, reserve target, value-system menu, settlement curves | **One numbers table** | The figures stay tunable by Board resolution. The machinery around them is not needed until a place has run for a year. |
| Clusters, federation across entities, affiliated entities, Custodial Council, Emergence annex | **One paragraph each** in the Rulebook | Architecture ahead of reality. Written when needed. |
| Partnership invoiced through a partner's Vehicle Entity | **Association invoices by default**; a partner's company may invoice and pay the slices as a service fee, if the Activity Sheet says so | Same money, one default path. |
| 22 annexes | **3 sheets** | Everything else was template or procedure. |
| General Terms, Internal Regulation, Operational Procedures, parameter register (19k words) | **One Rulebook (~3k words)** | One home for every rule. |
| Introduction, Overview, Cheatsheet, FAQ, Fund in Plain Language, two Covenants, Passport doc, Participant Charter (19k words) | **One Charter** | 2.0 is written to be read directly. |
| Regenerative Impact Framework, Safeguarding Policy | **Kept as they are**, by pointer | They are policies, not the stack. |

Two recent decisions are kept, each in a short paragraph of the Rulebook: the rule that members in residence always outnumber guests at a place, and the support arrangement (the network helps a person build a livelihood; part of the value returns when it comes).

## What is not negotiable

Italian law fixes the floor, and 2.0 keeps it: the Association is a social promotion association (APS) under the Third Sector Code; it never distributes profit or patrimony to anyone; membership is open and every member has one vote; volunteers are not paid; resources are lent for free (comodato); if the Association ends, what it owns passes to another mission entity. None of the simplifications above touch these.
