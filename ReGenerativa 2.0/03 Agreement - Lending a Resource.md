# Agreement - Lending a Resource

*Between the **Owner** and **ReGenerativa APS** (the Association). Reads with the [[02 Rulebook]]; the Rulebook is part of this agreement. The [[07 Sheets|Resource Sheet]] attached describes the place or asset you lend; both are called the resource below.*

---

## 1. What you lend

You lend the resource described in the Resource Sheet to the Association, for free, for the network's purpose as stated in the Sheet. You keep owning it. The Association uses it, cares for it, and returns it when the loan ends.

## 2. What the Association promises

- To use the resource only for the purpose and within the conditions on the Resource Sheet, and to keep it sound. Ordinary upkeep is ours; we keep a care log and share it with you on request.
- To have at least one steward caring for it under [[05 Agreement - Caring for a Resource]], and to bring the resource into use within the time in Rulebook section 11.
- To make no structural change without your written consent.
- To insure the activities held there or done with it, and to respect any exclusion or required access you name for a specific person, with reasons, which stays reviewable under Rulebook section 9.
- To send you, each year, the short note the steward sends the Board (Rulebook 6.6), so you see how the resource is doing.
- To return a share of every euro earned with the resource to the resource itself (the resource slice, Rulebook 4.2), so that using your gift rebuilds it rather than draining it.

## 3. What you promise

- Structural repairs and major systems (for a place) or major overhauls (for an asset) remain yours, unless the Resource Sheet records that the resource's reserve line may co-fund them.
- You give the network the access you have agreed and do not interfere with its use within the Sheet.
- You declare that you are entitled to lend the resource, and that it is safe for the stated use.

## 4. Money

Nothing is paid for the use of the resource, in any direction. Any co-funding of works from the resource's reserve line is recorded on the Resource Sheet, with a repayment schedule if the loan ends early. Earnings with the resource follow Rulebook section 4; the resource's envelope, its reserve, and its slice are described there.

## 5. Toward the commons

You and the Association record the shared intention that the resource may, in time, pass into the Association's permanent ownership: bought at the price or method on the Resource Sheet, funded by the resource's reserve line, or given. You are never obliged to sell or give. Once owned by the Association, the resource can only ever pass to another mission entity (Rulebook 2.4).

## 6. Ending

Either of us may end this agreement without cause with the notice in Rulebook section 11. Confirmed stays and activities within the notice are honoured; resident stewards are told and helped (Rulebook 8.1). If you sell, you tell us with the notice in section 11 and offer the buyer the chance to continue. Either of us may end at once for serious cause (Rulebook 8.5). The agreement ends automatically if the Association is dissolved (Rulebook 10).

## 7. Legal footing

Gratuitous loan for use, Art. 1803 and following of the Civil Code. The Association is a social promotion association under Legislative Decree 117/2017; it cannot pay rent, cannot distribute anything to anyone, and its patrimony is bound to its mission. Governing law, disputes, data protection, and force majeure: Rulebook sections 9 and closing note.
